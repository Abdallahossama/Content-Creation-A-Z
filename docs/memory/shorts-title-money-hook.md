**Why:** a concrete money figure + a promised problem pulls and holds better than "why people did X"; topic also differs, so
treat it as a strong hint, not proof.
**How to apply:** news Shorts titles lead with the money/number stake; if a Short is flat after ~1 day, retitle before giving
up. Related: [[shorts-standalone-only]], [[target-usa-canada]].
