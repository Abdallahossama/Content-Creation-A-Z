v1 was "super horrible". v5 was "the whole video is very good". The full approved recipe is in `docs/13-TALKING-HEAD-REEL-EDIT.md`.

Hard rules learned:
- Takes: eyes on camera and real energy; never reading from the phone.
- Cuts: no gaps or silences between pieces, and no phrase said twice.
- Zoom: no animated punch-in ("very ugly"); only a smooth, slow push.
- Colour: every clip matches the hook (he noticed the first seconds were darker).
- Stickers: above the head, not on the beard.
- Music: not chill/"cooking" (Late Night Radio), not anxious synth (Rising Tide). Eternity is approved, quiet (about 16 dB under the voice), no ducking.
- B-roll: not looking at the lens, at least 2.2 s.
- Always deliver a CapCut project.

**Why:** these were his exact complaints across the rounds.
**How to apply:** follow doc 13 for every talking-head reel; ask before changing the CTA card and the blurred-screen writing shot (he said keep them).
