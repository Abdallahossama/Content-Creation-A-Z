**How to apply:** use this structure for every reel; add only one orange #FF4F2B word per caption (his brand); copy the style, never the other creator's footage or words.

**Pipeline that worked (6 Nov, first reel video_20261105_130210):** phone clip is 1920x1080 with rotation → ffmpeg autorotates to 1080x1920. Raw audio very quiet (~-29 dB): loudnorm it before Whisper. Use cached `medium` in HF_HOME=E:/AskHabibiAI/hf (C: has <1 GB free → big downloads and beam>1 fail with mkl_malloc); transcribe in pause-split chunks or Whisper merges retakes. Retakes = same line said several times; keep the LAST complete take. Grade = eq contrast 1.07 sat 0.97 + colorbalance shadows teal/highlights warm + gentle S-curve + vignette PI/6. Voice = highpass 85, afftdn, EQ -2 dB@200 +3 dB@3.2k, compressor 3:1, loudnorm -14. Captions: full Alexandria ExtraBold TTF from Google Fonts CSS (fontsource subsets lose glyphs) and `ass=...:shaping=complex` (simple shaping shows boxes for Arabic isolated forms). Scripts lived in the session scratch folder (edit/build.py, plan.json).

**User feedback 6 Nov:** teal-orange grade made him look "very yellow and dark" → use the brighter neutral grade (eq brightness .025 gamma 1.10 contrast 1.09 sat .96, colorbalance mids/highs slightly cool, no warm highlight push). Always deliver TWO audio versions: chill music (Incompetech "Dream Culture", CC BY credit, -17 dB + sidechain duck) and HALAL = no instruments (voice + SFX + room_tone only). B-roll = his own project screenshots + brand cards (Edge headless screenshots of HTML, `[hidden]{display:none!important}`), face dimmed 45% under cards, whoosh/pop SFX from D:\why habibi\_style_kit\sfx.
