For Ask Habibi video graphics, never use the generic AI-design look: wordmark with a coloured dot, tracked ALL-CAPS
micro labels, mono "//" labels, rounded cards with thin borders, pills/badges, amber glow, light leaks, grain-for-mood,
duotone posters, icon illustrations. The user recognised it immediately as "how Claude itself designs things" and
called it AI slop (2026-10-12, Ep 2 "Midnight Noir" style frames).

**Why:** the channel should feel adult, funny and edited by a human; the generic look makes it read as AI content.

**How to apply:** use the style kit `D:\why habibi\_style_kit\` (README + STYLE_RULES.md, "cut & marker"; the user
loved it and made it the default on 2026-10-12): real things on screen (study pages via tools/receipt.py, Pexels photos,
imgflip memes) + an editor's hand on top (yellow highlighter, red/yellow hand-drawn marker, Ink Free notes), Rubik
captions with black outline, hard cuts and punch-ins. Mascot = Batata v2, a REAL potato photo with a marker-drawn face
(user asked for "less cartoon"); `cool` (marker sunglasses) is the brand face. Keep navy/yellow/red. Applies to all
channel design work incl. thumbnails, Shorts and brand assets. See [[ask-habibi-capcut-workflow]].
