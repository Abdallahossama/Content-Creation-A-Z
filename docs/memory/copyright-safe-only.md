Everything in Ask Habibi videos, thumbnails and Shorts must be copyright-safe (user rule, 2026-10-12, Ep 2): Pexels
photos/B-roll, our own Batata v2 + brand, home-made memes (meme formats rebuilt with Batata/Pexels/marker),
`_music_library` (CC BY with credit) or YouTube Audio Library, our own/CC0 SFX, study pages limited to title + 1–2
cited lines, OFL/system fonts. No imgflip templates, film/TV/celebrity stills, commercial music.

**How to apply:** follow the "Copyright-safe only" table in `D:\why habibi\_style_kit\STYLE_RULES.md`, keep a
`CREDITS.md` (asset, source, licence) per episode, and say so when something is fair-use quotation rather than
licensed. See [[no-ai-slop-design]], [[ask-habibi-capcut-workflow]].
