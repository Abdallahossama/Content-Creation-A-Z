**Audience.**
- Devices: TV 66%.
- Countries: US 34%, Canada 4%, Egypt 3%.
- Age 35+, 63% male.

**Long videos.** Average view 6:59. Traffic: Suggested 64%, Search 1.6%.
- Alcatraz: 368 views, 3.0% CTR.
- Franklin: 234 views, 60.7% viewed.
- Cooper: 169 views, 3.2% CTR.
- Roanoke: 1.6% CTR on 2.7K impressions. Fix this one first.
- Saddam: CTR 4.3–5.0%. 92% of its views come from Suggested, next to big Saddam videos. Half the viewers leave in minute 1.
- Muslim Brotherhood: 27 impressions. It was hidden at launch, then made public again after El-Sayed won.

**Shorts.** 33.6% stayed to watch.
- The Alcatraz "sleeping man's head" Short: 492 views, 80% viewed.
- The Alcatraz "noise" Short: 102% viewed, 3 subs (half of all subs).
- The Cooper Shorts flopped (5.5% viewed).
