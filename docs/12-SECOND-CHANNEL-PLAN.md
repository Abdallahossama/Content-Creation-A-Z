## Why change
- Goal of the second channel = income. US viewers first (highest RPM), then Canada, Europe.
- Ask Habibi long videos all stayed under 50 views (scattered topics, no audience match). Production stopped 30 Oct 2026.
- What DID work on Ask Habibi: money-number news Shorts. "France Owes EUR 3.6 Trillion" hit 1.7K views and 75.7% viewed. "Diesel Costs $2 More Than Gas" hit 1.4K in 24 h and 77.6% viewed.
- So the money angle has proof. The broken parts were the brand and the topic spread.

## The two reference videos (owner's links)
1. **Grow with Alex, "How To Start A YouTube Automation Business in 10 Minutes"** (VjpY2cETzy4, May 2023, 1.2M views)
   - Basic and from 2023: learn CPM vs RPM, CTR and watch time.
   - Start solo with free tools, not freelancers.
   - The workflow: pick a niche, study competitors' Popular tab and Social Blade, then make the video with ChatGPT script + free TTS + Pixabay stock + CapCut + Canva.
   - Niches it rates good: business/finance, philosophy, education, marketing, tech, relationships, crypto.
   - Niches to avoid: meditation, rain sounds, reactions, comedy/movie compilations, top-10, sports.
   - Its warnings: people fail from burnout, wrong tools and inconsistency. Don't chase a niche only for its RPM; 500K views in a fair niche beats 10K in a crowded one. YouTube rewards retention, CTR, watch time and session time.
   - Outdated in 2026: "ChatGPT writes the whole script + stock footage" is exactly the template content YouTube now demonetizes (see Policy below).
2. **Isaac, "I Blew Up a Shorts Channel in 7 Days!"** (QXfugR3ZIAs, 3 Oct 2026, 838K views)
   - He scrolled Shorts for research. Apart from AI slop, fake reddit stories and brainrot, the niche that kept winning was **business stories**: "why this company does this weird thing" (a short Magnates Media).
   - Day 1 flopped. The topic was depressing, the hook had no stakes, and there was no payoff.
   - The fix: borrow the *structure* of a viral script (not the words), then make a better hook and a real ending. The Brazil-prison-books Short reached 21K in 24 h with an 80% swipe rate and 96% retention.
   - Yamaha "makes everything" flopped. Same script structure, but his visuals (movie clips + Canva) lost to Zack D Films' animation.
   - Final script formula, copied from a fast-growing psychology channel:
     - (1) put the viewer INSIDE the story with a problem ("You're a huge coffee company and you have a problem");
     - (2) reveal the solution step by step;
     - (3) end on the real name/reveal (Nescafe in Japan).
   - Ending: no 1M views in 7 days. His point: copying for views felt empty; caring about the videos was the old "secret".
   - Notes for us:
     - He used movie scenes from YouTube, which is NOT copyright-safe for us.
     - Shorts reach is a lottery. Even his 21K hit earns almost nothing (see Shorts RPM below).

## Launch plan
- **Format:** 12-18 min long video, one American everyday thing, one money mechanism.
  - Script beats (yt-script):
    1. Cold open puts the viewer inside the problem with a money number (Isaac's formula: "You're the owner of a Waffle House and...").
    2. The hidden mechanism in 3-4 steps, each with a chart or map receipt.
    3. The "so what" for the viewer's wallet.
    4. Payoff, then a 2-video end screen.
  - Every video must have:
    - its own research, 5+ dated sources on screen;
    - varied structure;
    - our charts/maps;
    - the narrator as storyteller, never an AI money "expert".
- **Rhythm:** 1 long a week (Wed 19:00 Cairo = 12 PM ET) + 2 Shorts a week (money-number, each linked to a live long video).
  - Last Hours keeps Sat/Sun.
  - YPP route = 4,000 long-form hours, not Shorts.
- **Titles:** "The Question" formula + a $ number. Examples: "Why a Pickup Truck Now Costs $65,000"; "How Waffle House Makes Money at 3 AM"; "Who Owns America's $36 Trillion Debt?". Under 55 characters, US units and $.
- **Thumbnails:**
  - one real object (truck, receipt, hot dog, carrier) cut out big on a clean colour field;
  - a price tag or red number (2-4 words max);
  - one small arrow or graph line;
  - no faces unless men only; no AI images; no potato.
- **Voice:** Chatterbox clone. Use a different reference voice from The Last Hours so the brands don't sound identical (owner to choose).
- **First 10 videos** (fact-check every number before scripting):
  1. Why a New Pickup Truck Now Costs $65,000
  2. Why Diesel Costs $2 More Than Gas (long version of our best Short)
  3. Who Actually Owns America's $36 Trillion Debt? (map)
  4. How Waffle House Never Closes and Still Makes Money
  5. Why Car Insurance Jumped So Fast in America
  6. What One Aircraft Carrier Really Costs (PD Navy photos)
  7. How the Panama Canal Makes Billions a Year (map)
  8. Why Department Stores Are Dying
  9. Why Your Electricity Bill Is Rising: AI Data Centers
  10. Why America Can't Build Ships Anymore (PD photos)
- **Judge** each video on day 4-7: CTR > 4%, avg view > 35%. Retitle flat ones after ~2 days.
- **Capacity risk:** the owner already makes 2 Last Hours files a week. If the new channel slows them, The Last Hours wins.
