## 3. Candidates (title.py)
1. **Is Your Attention Span Really 8 Seconds?** (100) The goldfish myth + brain rot + scrolling in bed.
   Receipts: the 8-second / goldfish line traces to a 2015 Microsoft Canada marketing report citing Statistic Brain;
   the BBC (Simon Maybin, 2017) found no research behind it. What is real: Nguyen et al. 2025, Psychological Bulletin,
   71 studies, 98,299 people: heavier short-form video use is linked with poorer attention (r = −.38) and inhibitory
   control (r = −.41), correlational. PMID 41231585.
2. **Does Mouth Taping Actually Work?** (94) Rhee et al. 2025, PLoS One: 10 studies, 213 patients, 2 showed
   improvement, "potentially serious risk of harm" when done indiscriminately (PMID 40397877); Alshehri et al. 2026,
   OTO Open meta-analysis (PMID 42662994). Thumbnail writes itself: Batata with tape over its mouth.
3. **Is Your Sleep Tracker Keeping You Awake?** (94) Orthosomnia, Baron et al. 2017, J Clin Sleep Med (PMID 27855740).
   Fewer studies; better as a section inside 2.
Evergreen backup: Why Do You Forget Why You Walked Into a Room? (80) Radvansky 2011 (PMID 21563019).
