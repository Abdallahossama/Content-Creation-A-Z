# Ep 4 (documentary special) research brief: "Why Do 2 Tiny Straits Control the World's Oil?"
Started 2026-10-12, 21:50 Cairo. Rule: every number gets a source and a date; nothing from Iranian state media alone;
aggregators (straits.live, hormuzstraitmonitor) only as pointers to the primary source.

## Hormuz (structural, EIA = US government, public domain)
- 2024: ~20 million b/d, ~20% of global petroleum liquids consumption; ~1/5 of global LNG trade (mostly Qatar);
  84% of the crude/condensate and 83% of the LNG went to Asia. Bypass pipelines (Saudi, UAE) ~2.6 million b/d spare.
  (EIA Today in Energy 65504)
- Width: ~21 miles at the narrowest; two 2-mile shipping lanes + a 2-mile buffer (EIA chokepoints page) -> VERIFY on EIA.

## The closure (2026, news; dated)
- Feb 28: US-Israel strikes on Iran; Hormuz largely blocked since (Al Jazeera, Wikipedia "2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis").
- Early March: war-risk insurance cancelled (P&I clubs) -> VERIFY primary (Lloyd's List / Reuters).
- Apr 8 ceasefire, short-lived; Apr 17 brief reopening, at least 13 tankers got out (CSIS "Hormuz in 8 charts").
- Oct 4: 4 transits vs ~85/day baseline (IMF PortWatch, via USNI/straits.live) -> VERIFY on PortWatch; note AIS undercount.
- Oct 1-6: nine ships struck (IMO count, USNI News Oct 9). Brent ~$104.7 (Oct 9 close).
- Oct 11: Trump says no longer interested in a deal (Al Jazeera Oct 11).
- US military shipping lane along Oman's coast (CNBC Sep 25) -> VERIFY.

## The escape route
- Saudi East-West pipeline (Abqaiq -> Yanbu): 5 million b/d historically; pushed to 7 million in March by converting NGL
  lines (Fortune Mar 28); 5.8 million b/d in early October after a September drone attack (Al Jazeera Oct 6).
- Yanbu loading ~4-4.5 million b/d in practice (analysis cited by Al Jazeera Sep 14/17) -> VERIFY.

## Bab el-Mandeb + the Houthis
- Oil flows: 8.7 million b/d in 2023 -> ~4.0 million b/d Jan-Aug 2024 after Houthi attacks; Cape of Good Hope 6 -> 9.2
  million b/d (EIA Today in Energy 63446).
- Width: ~18 miles at the narrowest (sources differ; VERIFY).
- Houthi attacks began Nov 2023; resumed July 2025; resumed against Saudi Arabia 13 July 2026.
- Sep 11-12, 2026: Houthis seized Mayun (Perim), 13 sq km, in Bab el-Mandeb (Al Jazeera, Euronews, NPR).
- Sep 14: Greater and Lesser Hanish (AP via PBS/ABC).
- Oct 2026: Saudi-backed counteroffensive; Houthi missiles on Riyadh's King Khalid airport (deaths reported, counts differ);
  Oct 11 Trump: US could join (NBC). Oct 5: Turkey-Pakistan-Saudi defence pact "deterrence measures" (Al Jazeera).
