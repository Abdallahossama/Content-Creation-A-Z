## Read
- Retention is the strength (avg view ~7 min on long videos); the click rate (2.6%) and subscriber conversion (6 subs / 2,179 views = 0.28%) are the weaknesses.
- Famous name + proven topic = the best click rate (Saddam 4.3-5.0%) and YouTube places it next to big channels. Lean into that.
- Roanoke wastes the most impressions (2,702 at 1.6%): first candidate for a new thumbnail/title.
- Saddam's first minute is the leak (half leave): every new file needs a faster first 60 s.
- Shorts: a concrete creepy moment in second 1 wins (Alcatraz Shorts 80-102%); slow or abstract openings die (Cooper $5,800: 5.5%).
