Wednesday, July 11, 1979, 2:30 in the afternoon: two men are shot dead inside a liquor store at Dadeland Mall, a family mall in the Miami suburbs, and the gunmen drive off in a van painted "Happy Time Complete Party Supply." Over the next seven years, Miami became the murder capital of America, the county morgue ran out of room, and it all ended on a quiet street on a Friday morning in 1986, in one of the deadliest gunfights in FBI history.

GTA's Vice City wasn't invented. It was copied. This is the real Miami behind it, from the files: the cash surplus the Federal Reserve noticed, Operation Greenback, the Mariel boatlift, the 1980 riots, the record year of 1981 and the "hamburger van" morgue, the South Florida Task Force run by the Vice President, the Miami River Cops, Scarface and Miami Vice, and the FBI Miami shootout of April 11, 1986, hour by hour.
