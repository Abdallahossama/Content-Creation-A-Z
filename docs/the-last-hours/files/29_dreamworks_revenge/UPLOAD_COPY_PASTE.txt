DESCRIPTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Easter Sunday, April 3, 1994: a helicopter goes down in Nevada's Ruby Mountains, and Disney's president, Frank Wells, is killed. The man running Disney's movie studio, Jeffrey Katzenberg, asks for his job. The answer is no. Within months he's gone, and with Steven Spielberg and David Geffen he builds DreamWorks, the studio that made Shrek, Kung Fu Panda and How to Train Your Dragon, won the first Oscar ever given for Best Animated Feature, and was sold to Comcast in 2016 for $3.8 billion.

This is the full story from the files: the promise, the lawsuit, the race between Antz and A Bug's Life, Shrek 2's record, the 8% Paramount fee, the write-downs, Katzenberg's big bets (3D, China, Netflix, YouTube), the 2015 layoffs and the sale. I'm Noah, the File Keeper.
