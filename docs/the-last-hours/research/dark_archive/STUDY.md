## The one thing the hits share
A famous Arab dictator (or his son) **doing it himself, on camera, in an ordinary room** (a party hall, a basketball court).
"Caught on camera" alone is not enough: Sadat and Rabin were filmed too and got 15-44K. The viewer clicks to see the man and the room.

## Title formula (a judgement on the title, not proof of why it worked)
"The Day [dictator] Turned [ordinary place] Into a Public [Purge / Execution]": the "ordinary place -> horror" contrast, the dictator's name first, no number, no question.

## Thumbnail formula
One real frame of the man himself (Saddam smiling with a cigar at the lectern), ONE red condensed word (PURGE), red-edged vignette, VHS colour bleed.
