2. RULES FOR EVERY NEW FILE
=====================================================================================================
OPENING (0:00-1:00)
 1. Frame 1: the hero image + on-screen text. Never a blank, a logo or a plain shot.
 2. Line 1: "Imagine you..." + a number + something unresolved. hookscore.py >= 70.
 3. Line 2-3: weekday, date, clock time.
 4. Prove the payoff by 0:30: say exactly what the viewer will know by the end.
 5. NO spoken "This is The Last Hours." and no pause. At most a 1.5 s visual stamp over moving footage while
    the voice keeps going.
 6. NO backstory block after the cold open. Go straight into the countdown. Context comes in 1-2 lines at a
    time, only at the moment the viewer needs it, never more than ~15 s of history in a row.

MIDDLE
 7. Open a new loop every 60-90 s ("Remember the coat. It matters later." / "Eight days earlier, he said
    something strange to his boss. We'll get to it.") and close each one later, on screen.
 8. A visual change every 4-6 s; no still image held longer than 8 s; real maps when the place changes.
 9. One idea per beat. If a chapter can't be named in 5 words it is two chapters or filler.
10. Our edge over the big channels: they repeat the legends; we show the real record, with dates, and say
    plainly which famous "clue" is fake. Myth vs record is a recurring beat, not a footnote.

FACTS (mistakes we already made: Short cards "2 OILSKINS GONE" / "A BOX, SMASHED", Alcatraz "dummy heads"
wording, Centralia "5 houses", Flannan coat details in the pinned comment)
11. Every number, quote and claim comes from research.md "safe lines"; run the do-not-say list against the
    final VO text AND every on-screen card, title, description, pinned comment and Short before rendering.

PACKAGING
12. Title: number + "vanished/gone" + one concrete object (word, coat, note). title.py >= 94. Searchable
    name in the title or the first line of the description.
13. Thumbnails: Hakawi colour, ONE real hero image filling the frame, 2 short text lines, big on a phone.
    Show the user and WAIT for approval before uploading or touching an A/B test. A/B test all 3.
14. Pinned comment the moment it goes public; end screen (2 videos + subscribe).
15. Teaser Shorts point (description + related video) to the long video they come from.

AUDIO / VISUAL HARD RULES
16. Natural-speed cloned voice, never time-stretched; float decode, no 8-bit joins; -14 LUFS.
17. No women or girls in any image. Real images only, no AI, no cartoons. CREDITS.md per video.

PRODUCTION MISTAKES 22-23 OCT (and the fix that is now the rule)
22. QUOTES: never put a quote over a document page (that is text over text). Quotes go on a dimmed PHOTO (cockpit, sea, room).
23. BLACK CARDS: every card/stat/clock card sits on ambient(k) = the nearest photo, lightly blurred + dimmed
    (_tools/no_black_patch.py). Before upload, run _tools/../07_egyptair/build/_blackscan.py on the export:
    no dark stretch over 1.5 s except the end screen and night photos that are dark on purpose.
24. OVERLAY TIMING BUG: an overlay must end at the EARLIEST later overlay in its slot, not the next one in list order
    (ov_end fix; an out-of-order entry put two captions on screen at once in EgyptAir 7:25). New OV lists: check with
    07_egyptair/build/_ovchk.py.
25. RENDER MEMORY: never run more than ~3 parallel segments for map-heavy renders, and never two videos at once.
    6-8 parallel map segments ran out of RAM and produced 1-2 s segments. Cooper needs `--with pyproj`.
    ALWAYS check every seg_*.mp4 duration and the final duration (ffprobe) before calling a render done.
26. CUTTING A FINISHED VIDEO: remove the ident / ask with _tools/cut_ranges.py (frame-accurate, 30 ms audio fades),
    then shift the chapter times and the end-screen start (both are in the upload sheet "V2" section).
27. RE-UPLOADS: YouTube cannot replace a file. Upload the new file, use "Reuse details" (title, description, playlist,
    tags, language, remixing, category) and End screen "Import from video" from the old upload, then set the A/B
    thumbnails, "not made for kids" (not copied) and the schedule; only then set the old upload Private and retitle it
    "OLD COPY (delete)". Deleting is the user's job. New video IDs mean: update the teaser Shorts' related video,
    pinned-comment notes and any end screen that points at the old ID.
28. FIRST-SECOND CHECK: the opening must say the title's promise; a short that opens on logistics (dates, boats, counts)
    loses half the viewers by 0:30. The spoken "This is The Last Hours" ident is gone for good.
29. BEFORE SAYING "DONE": watch the frame sheet of the FINAL export (not only QA frames) at the start, each segment
    seam, every changed beat and the end screen.
30. FIX A PUBLIC VIDEO WITHOUT LOSING VIEWS: YouTube Studio > Editor > Trim and cut (times are mm:ss:frames on the ORIGINAL
    timeline; a new cut is created in the gap around the playhead, so move the playhead first). Then shift the description
    chapters (subtract each removed span). Used 23 Oct: Alcatraz (ident 0:38.6-0:41.1 + ask 5:26-5:40), Roanoke (ident
    0:55.2-0:57.7), Ask Habibi Clothes ("I'm Habibi" 0:23-0:26.5). Processing takes a few hours; the video stays up.
31. PRINT-CARD SHOTS (PRINTS kind) used to start on plain black until the cards drop in: Andrée had 1.5-2 s black at 0:42 and
    13:19 (found by _blackscan.py on the export). Fixed in render_andree.py: the cards drop onto the middle print, blurred.
    Copy that frame() change into every new renderer.
32. ARCTIC / POLE MAPS: mshorts' Mercator map stops at 84°N. Use 08_andree/polarmap.py (north-up polar stereographic,
    Natural Earth, great-circle routes, "dist" lines with mile labels); it matched the research distances exactly.
33. VO FIXES: run fix_lines.py "pick" ONCE per batch (a second run re-splices and overwrites the _v1 backup). Whisper flags
    numbers as misreads ("146" for "one forty-six"); only re-voice real word errors and end-of-line babble.
34. DARK SEA BACKGROUNDS: Pexels night-sea photos (E1/E2-type) under a list/quote/counter read as BLACK on a phone (_blackscan flagged 2-8 s
    stretches in Flight 19). Put text on a brighter real photo (the ship, the plane, the man, foam-white sea) and keep dim <= 0.3.
35. CHATTERBOX: run it with HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 (it hung on a network check) and never beside OCR/renders (paging-file crash, 3x slower).
    Whisper's initial_prompt biases numbers ("Four twenty-one" -> "Flight 2421"): re-check flagged lines WITHOUT the prompt before re-voicing.
36. DOC SPOTLIGHTS: OCR every page once (ocr_docs.py, RapidOCR) and key each mark to OCR row indices ("12+13"); the "boxes" mode prints
    the text under every spotlight. Rows OCR missed: give a page-pixel box by hand.
37. SUBSCRIBE ASKS (user + research, 24 Oct): mid-video = ONE silent visual badge ("SUBSCRIBE FOR THE NEXT FILE", 4 s) at 60-80% of the
    video, right after a payoff, at a calm break (never before the climax; never spoken: Alcatraz dropped after the spoken ask). End = one
    spoken line AFTER the comment question: "If you want the next file, subscribe." (or "...that file..." when the next file was named).
    The channel watermark (Studio > Customization > Branding) covers every video, scheduled and public.
38. RESEARCH CLIP CHECKS ARE NOT ENOUGH (file 10, 24 Oct): the asset list said "men only" for every clip, but DVIDS D4 had a woman soldier at
    2:30 and a gutted-hall shot had a tiny figure. Before using ANY clip, make a 3-5 s contact sheet of the exact seconds used (ffmpeg fps+tile
    with timestamps) and check by eye; use only seconds with no person, or men only. Same for photos (one Commons photo had a long-haired
    figure from behind: dropped).
39. MOVING FOOTAGE (vidclip.py, "vid"/"vidp" shots): Pexels titles lie ("dark auditorium" = a cinema with a screen; "CRT" clips flicker to
    black; night clips read as black). Grade with the renderer's photo grade, lift dark clips (LIFT gamma), crop out logos/screens, and keep
    176-px DVIDS clips as small prints (vidp), never full frame. Cut keys never on a line's last word (<1 s flashes): "keys" mode lists them.
40. QUOTE THE PAGE YOU SHOW: the same Iraqi statement says "national interest" in FBIS's summary (E1) and "pan-Arab interest" in the full
    text (E2). Spotlight the page whose words match the narration, and read the page image yourself before scripting a quote.
41. SHORT TITLES THAT WIN (Studio, 25 Oct): the Alcatraz teaser "He Touched the Sleeping Man's Head. It Rolled Off." got 139 views and 51.4%
    viewed within hours, more than any long file. Formula: two short sentences, a concrete physical action by one person, then a shocking
    result; no date, no logistics. Long files keep their viewers (Alcatraz 47.2% average view) but get few impressions: teasers feed them.
42. NEW FILES 15-16 (30 Oct): build render_<file>.py from the newest branded engine with a small _build_render.py that swaps blocks
    (paths, TB tags, CROP, SHOTS/OV from shots_<file>.py, CAPFIX, music, EV, CH, ENDL) instead of hand-editing 900 lines.
    Scanned PDFs: render pages to media/<rid>p<n>_page.jpg and OCR once (_tools/ocr_pages.py); find marks with _tools/ocr_find.py.
    PDFs with a text layer (Utah newspapers, NHHC book): build the OCR json from the words (exact boxes). Mark DOC spots by OCR row
    numbers ("69+70"). Cards with red ink: COLORDOC (page_img keeps colour) so the "ESCAPED" stamp stays red.
43. MEMORY: Chatterbox segfaulted / "paging file too small" when it ran beside OCR while WSL held ~6 GB. Run voice jobs alone in a
    chain script (_tools/chain_vo*.sh) and check free memory (Win32_OperatingSystem) before starting.
44. Whisper writes digits ("1,195", "900"): word keys must use the digit form too ("1195|eleven"), or the shot falls back to the line start.

45. RENDER IN PARTS, ONE AT A TIME (31 Oct): parallel "all n" renders ran out of memory and wrote short parts. _tools/render_seq.sh
    renders part i alone, checks its length with ffprobe, retries up to 3 times, then runs audio + final. Indianapolis (8 parts) and Cole & Roe
    (5) finished clean this way.
46. A FILE NAME IS NOT THE PICTURE (31 Oct, Gaddafi): the VOA Bani Walid set (A17-A22) held different frames than assets.md described
    (the "shell casings" file was a fighter's face). It nearly put a young fighter's face under "he was beaten and abused". Check every
    still on the contact sheet (_tools/stills_sheet.py) and tag it by what is SEEN, never by the download note.
47. QA THE WHOLE CUT BEFORE THE RENDER: `render qa` (one mid-frame per shot) caught a 0.45-degree Natural Earth map that showed nothing
    (too coarse below ~2 degrees: use a gulf-scale map or a COUNTER), a burned-in title in an EC clip (V10a), dark smoke clips that would
    read as black, a counter whose number fell into the next shot, and the end card followed by a stray shot. LIFT is a gamma: below 1 lifts.
48. THUMBNAIL GLOW (31 Oct): blur a glow on a PADDED canvas, or its box edge shows as a square (owner spotted it on "ONE AFRICA").
    Faces in a bubble stay in natural colour over a toned background (owner: contrast).
