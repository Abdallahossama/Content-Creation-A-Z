### Why Saddam won (and how to repeat it)
- **It rides a huge existing ecosystem.** 87% of views come from Suggested, beside big Iraq/Saddam videos: "The Blue Car That Led America to Zarqawi" 26.4%, "The Brutal Last Hours of Saddam Hussein" 22.2%, "The Lie That Got Saddam Hussein Killed" 9.7%, "248 Days on the Run... The Hunt for Saddam Hussein" 9.4%, "The Day Saddam Hussein Turned the Hall Into a Public Purge" 3.4%.
- **It has the best CTR on the channel**: 3.6% overall and 6.1% on Browse. That comes from the famous face and name plus a concrete number ("66 names").
- **It accelerates instead of fading.** Days 1-3: 1 view a day. Day 4 (5 Nov): 81. It has held 60-66 a day since. Alcatraz's push died after about 2 weeks (4 views in 48 h).
- **It does NOT win on retention.** It holds 38% vs Alcatraz 55%. Retention (still watching): 0:40 69%, 1:20 57%, 2:00 50%, 3:30 40%, 7:00 29%, end 22%. Alcatraz: 0:50 71%, 2:30 63%, 5:00 59%, 10:00 49%, end 35%. Saddam bleeds steadily from 0:40 to 3:30, so the setup is too slow for the war-doc viewers YouTube sends.
- **Title A/B test finished (1-14 Nov), watch-time share:**
  - "66 Names Were Read Out. 21 Men Were Gone in 17 Days." = **48.0%**
  - "Saddam Hussein's 1979 Purge: 66 Names, One by One" = 30.4%
  - "Saddam Hussein Called a Meeting..." = 21.6%, but Studio still shows this title as live.
- **Rule:** pick famous people/events that already have million-view videos (the Suggested ecosystem), and put a concrete number plus a consequence in the title. A new video needs about 4-7 days before the push starts.
