## Licence key
- **PD**: public domain by age (published or made 1845-1906; every creator died long ago).
  - Faithful scans of PD flat works (maps, prints, documents) are PD on Commons ("PD-Art").
  - For Royal Museums Greenwich (RMG) items, take the **Commons copy**: RMG's own site licenses images for non-commercial use only. Credit "National Maritime Museum, Greenwich".
- **PD (NASA) / PD (USGS Landsat)**: US government works. Free for commercial use; don't use the NASA logo.
- **Public Domain Mark**: the owner marked it PD on Flickr.
- **CC0**: no rights reserved.
- **No known restrictions**: British Library / Internet Archive Book Images Flickr sets (scans from out-of-copyright books).
- **Pexels licence**: free commercial use, attribution optional.
- **CC BY** (usable with a credit line) and **CC BY-SA** (avoid unless essential): only in section H, flagged in bold.

## Hard rules applied (everything below passed them)
- **No women or girls.** Every image was checked by eye: daguerreotypes, engravings (including crowds), photos and Pexels. Rejects are listed at the end.
- **Real only.** Photos, documents, period engravings and maps (two period oil paintings sit in the alternates, flagged). No AI, no modern illustrations, nothing from AMC's *The Terror* or any film/TV.
- **No human remains or bodies.** The Beechey exhumation photos, the skeleton finds, and the dying-men painting from The Graphic (1896) were all left out.

## Access notes
- **Wikimedia Commons**:
  - Originals on `upload.wikimedia.org` answer 200 but rate-limit bursts. A test today gave 2 OK and 1 **HTTP 429**. Download one file at a time, with a few seconds' pause and a real User-Agent.
  - Files wider than 1920 px use the API's 1920 px `thumburl` on `thumb.wikimedia.org`; all of these returned 200.
  - For TIFF masters, that URL is a JPEG render.
- **Internet Archive**:
  - `https://archive.org/download/<id>/page/n<N>.jpg` is the full-resolution page.
  - For a 1920-wide copy, put `_w1920` before `.jpg`, e.g. `.../page/n7_w1920.jpg`.
  - The ILN items are microfilm scans of whole newspaper pages: crop the engraving.
- **Library of Congress**: loc.gov returned Cloudflare 403 from this connection, so it was not searched.
