In 1845, 129 men sailed into the Canadian Arctic with Sir John Franklin. Not one came home. The only message that tells us what happened to them is a single sheet of paper left in a stone cairn, with two words on it: All well.

This is the story of that paper, from the real records: the three graves on Beechey Island, the ships locked in the ice, the 105 men who started walking, what Inuit hunters told John Rae, the boat full of silver and chocolate, the wrecks of HMS Erebus and HMS Terror found in 2014 and 2016, the DNA that is giving the men their names back, and the door on Terror that is still closed.
