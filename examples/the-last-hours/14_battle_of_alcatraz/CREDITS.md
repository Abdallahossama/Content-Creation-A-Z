# File 14: the Battle of Alcatraz (2-4 May 1946): credits

Real images only. Nothing shown of the dead, the wounded or the morgue; no women on screen (the 1946 newsreel is cut to the
seconds without onlookers). Only the items below appear in the edit (render_alcatraz46.py SHOTS).

## National Park Service, Golden Gate NRA Park Archives (public domain / NPS "Full" rights)
- James V. Bennett, "The Alcatraz Revolt", reprinted from The Bulletin Board, July 1946: "A Tribute to William A. Miller and Harold P. Stites"
  (GOGA 18308a) and the drawing "Detail at gun gallery / Detail of spreader bar" (report p. 6).
- United Press telegram to Warden J. A. Johnston, 2 May 1946 (GOGA 18308b).
- Museum objects: the bar spreader (GOGA 18261b), bullets recovered after the battle (GOGA 18629, 32232), an Alcatraz Thompson gun, Model 1928 (GOGA 55).
- Photographs: Bernard Coy, Alcatraz No. 415 (GOGA-2316); Joseph Cretzer (GOGA-2316f); smoke from the cell house, May 1946 (GOGA 18261d);
  an officer at the gun-gallery bars (GOGA 18341a); Johnston and Bennett meet the press (GOGA 18341b); Carnes, Shockley and Thompson on the
  way to court (GOGA 18261i); officers on the bay patrol (GOGA 18261e); Clarence Carnes, 1980; Alcatraz officers before the Administration
  Building; a tower guard's rifles, c. 1940; Alcatraz from the air, 1950 (Col. Werner E. Michel); the east side; the cell house, west side;
  "Broadway", 1963; the utility corridor, 1962.
- Plan of the cell house, 1949 (NPS, via Wikimedia Commons).
