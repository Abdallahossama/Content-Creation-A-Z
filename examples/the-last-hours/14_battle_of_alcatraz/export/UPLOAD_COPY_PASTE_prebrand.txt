DESCRIPTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Battle of Alcatraz, Thursday, May 2 to Saturday, May 4, 1946, hour by hour from the court record, the Bureau of Prisons' own report and the 1946 wire reports. Six convicts got two guns out of the gun gallery and locked nine officers in two cells. They needed one more thing: key 107, to the yard door. They never found it.

You'll see the Bureau of Prisons' drawing of the gun gallery and the bar spreader, the warden's notebook cards for all six men, the U.S. Attorney's brief ("Miller told him, 107"), the United Press wire to the warden, and what the Marine who did the firing actually described. Sixteen years later, three men left the same cell house through a utility corridor: that file is on this channel.

An educational documentary built from the federal court record, Bureau of Prisons and National Park Service archives, and 1946 newspapers. No images of the dead or the wounded are shown.
