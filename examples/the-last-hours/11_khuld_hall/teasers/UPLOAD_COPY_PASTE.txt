Each Name Read Out Meant a Man Was Walked Out of the Hall #saddam

Sunday, July 22, 1979. Baghdad. Saddam Hussein has been president for six days. A man at a microphone confesses to a plot, and as each name is read, that man is ordered to stand and is walked out by guards. Saddam chairs the meeting, smoking a cigar. And the whole meeting is being filmed.

The full file, hour by hour: "Saddam Hussein Called a Meeting. 66 Names Were Read Out." on this channel.

An FBI Agent Watched Saddam's 1979 Tape. This Is What He Saw #saddam

The tape names about sixty-six men. Fear, the FBI agent wrote, was the most noticeable emotion. One by one, each named man was escorted out of the hall. Then a name was read that made Saddam cry: the director of his own office.
