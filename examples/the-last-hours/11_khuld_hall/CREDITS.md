# The Last Hours, file 10: the al-Khuld Hall purge (Baghdad, July-August 1979): credits

## Narration
The creator's own voice, cloned locally (Chatterbox Turbo, MIT). Script: `vo_lines.txt`; every fact from `research/research.md`
(safe lines section 16, do-not-say list section 17). Quotes are U.S. government text (State Department cables, FRUS, FBI interview report,
FBIS translations of Iraqi broadcasts) or one short attributed line from Al-Ahram (as translated by FBIS). Numbers: "about 66" names is
the FBI agent's count (other sources: about 50; 68 tried); 22 sentenced to death, 21 executed.

## The 1979 film of the meeting: NOT USED
No footage or still of the 22 July 1979 meeting appears (research.md section 18: U.S. status unprovable, possible Iraqi protection,
Content ID risk from documentaries that contain it). The hall scene is told with the documents, the FBI agent's description and texture.
