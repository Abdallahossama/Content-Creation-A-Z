# The Last Hours, File 04: Roanoke, John White's return (15-19 August 1590): credits

## The record (public domain)
- **John White, "The fift voyage of M. John White into the West Indies and parts of America called Virginia, in the yeere 1590"**, and his letter to Richard Hakluyt (4 Feb 1593), printed in Richard Hakluyt, *The Principal Navigations, Voiages, Traffiques and Discoueries of the English Nation*, vol. 3 (London, 1600), pp. 283-295. Page images: Wellcome Collection copy, via the Internet Archive. All quotes on screen are from this printing.
- John White's account of the 1587 voyage and the 1587 names list (same volume).

## Reconstructions
The carved "C R O" and "CROATOAN" shots are **reconstructions** (our own lettering on a real bark photo, labelled on screen): the original tree and post do not survive. The "agreed sign" card follows White's own description of the code.

## Facts
Every date, time and quote: `research/research.md`. Main sources: Hakluyt (1600) as above; the National Park Service (Fort Raleigh NHS pages, incl. "Major Theories", updated 2025-2026); Encyclopedia Virginia; NCpedia; William Strachey, *The Historie of Travell into Virginia Britania* (c. 1612); John Smith's *True Relation* (1608) and *Generall Historie* (1624); John Lawson, *A New Voyage to Carolina* (1709); Stahle et al., *Science* (1998), the tree-ring drought record; First Colony Foundation (2012 map patch; "The Lost Colony is Found!", 4 Jun 2026); Croatoan Archaeological Society / Mark Horton and Scott Dawson (Hatteras finds and the June 2026 radiocarbon claim, reported in the press); Ewen & Shields, *Becoming the Lost Colony* (2024).
"About 115" colonists: our count of the 1587 names list (119 English names, minus White, Fernandes and George Howe); published totals run from 115 to 118. "About five in the morning": computed daybreak for 18 Aug 1590 (Old Style). Strachey's and Lawson's reports are labelled hearsay or reports; the 2026 claims are labelled claims (not peer reviewed).
