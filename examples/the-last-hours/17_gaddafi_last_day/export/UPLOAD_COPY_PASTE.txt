DESCRIPTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sirte, Libya, Thursday, October 20, 2011: at about 8:30 in the morning, NATO aircraft hit a convoy of about fifty vehicles trying to break out of the city. In it was Muammar Gaddafi. By eleven he was in the hands of the fighters hunting him. Within hours he was dead. This is the file, hour by hour, from the record.

You'll see why the convoy left in daylight instead of in the dark, whose aircraft struck it (and what NATO said it did not know), the crown he claimed in 2008 as "king of kings" of Africa and his call for one African army of a million soldiers "to confront NATO", the drainage pipes under the road, the grenade that bounced back, what the UN and Human Rights Watch found about his capture, and the questions no court has ever answered. No images of the capture or of the dead are shown.

An educational documentary built from the UN Commission of Inquiry on Libya (2012), Human Rights Watch's "Death of a Dictator" (2012), NATO's own statements, U.S. government records and contemporary reporting.
