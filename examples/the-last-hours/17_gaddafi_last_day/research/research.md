# MUAMMAR GADDAFI'S LAST DAY: verified research (Sirte, Libya, Thursday 20 October 2011, about 8:00 a.m. Libyan time: a convoy of about 50 vehicles breaks out of District 2; about 8:30 a.m.: NATO aircraft strike it; about 11 a.m.: Misrata fighters take him alive beside two drainage pipes under a road; by the time an ambulance reaches Misrata, about two hours away, he is dead; Friday 21 - Monday 24 October: his body lies in a meat locker in Misrata; Tuesday 25 October, about 5 a.m.: buried in a secret grave in the desert; the self-image behind it all: Thursday 28 August 2008, Benghazi: "king of kings" of Africa)

Channel: The Last Hours (@TheLastHoursDocs; US / Canada / UK audience). Format: hour-by-hour countdown with the clock times the record has, told only from the record. Folder 17_gaddafi_last_day. Narrator: the File Keeper. Earlier Gaddafi file: 12_gaddafi_stadium (1984; background, photo rights and the Libyan-copyright note reused here, story not repeated).

Spine: **Thu 9 Sep 1999** in Sirte, the OAU decides "to establish an African Union", inspired, the declaration says, by "the important proposals submitted by Colonel Muammar Ghaddafi" → **Thu 28 Aug 2008, "late on Thursday", Benghazi** more than 200 African kings and traditional rulers name him "king of kings"; he asks them for "an African military to defend Africa" → **Mon 2 Feb 2009, Addis Ababa** elected chairman of the African Union (a one-year rotating post) → **Wed 23 Sep 2009** at the UN he speaks "in the name of ... one thousand traditional African kingdoms" → **Tue 14 Dec 2010, Dakar** "Africa should have one army with one million soldiers", to "confront NATO, China, France, Britain" → **Feb 2011** uprising → **Thu 17 Mar** UN resolution 1973 → **Thu 31 Mar** NATO takes command (Operation Unified Protector) → **Thu 21 Apr** Washington approves armed Predator drones over Libya → **Sun 21 - Tue 23 Aug** Tripoli falls; he flees to Sirte, his hometown → **from Fri 16 Sep** Sirte under assault; he moves house every four or five days; "no communication, no television, nothing" → **Tue 18 Oct** in Tripoli the US Secretary of State says she hopes he "can be captured or killed soon" → **Thu 20 Oct 2011: ~3:30-4:00 a.m.** planned break-out; **8:00 a.m.** the convoy leaves, late → **~8:30 a.m. (2:30 a.m. in Washington)** NATO aircraft hit it; a missile strikes the car in front of his; the airbags go off → a second NATO strike, two 500-lb bombs → his son Mutassim leaves to find vehicles → on foot, about 100 meters (330 ft) to drainage pipes under the main road → a bodyguard's grenade bounces back off the concrete; his defence minister dies; he is cut on the left temple → a white flag made from a turban → **~11 a.m.** captured alive by Misrata fighters: "What is going on?" → beaten and abused; put in an ambulance → **at least two hours to Misrata**; seemingly dead on arrival → **~4:30-4:50 p.m. (~10:30-10:50 a.m. in Washington)** Libya's interim prime minister: "Muammar Gaddafi has been killed" → afternoon: his son Mutassim, also captured alive, is dead → **2:07 p.m. EDT (8:07 p.m. Libya)** President Obama in the Rose Garden → **Fri 21 Oct** NATO: "At the time of the strike, NATO did not know that Qadhafi was in the convoy" → **21-24 Oct** bodies on public display in a Misrata meat locker → **Tue 25 Oct, ~5 a.m.** father, son and defence minister buried at a secret desert site → **Mon 31 Oct, 23:59 Libyan time** NATO's mission ends → **Mar 2012** UN inquiry: cannot confirm whether his death was an unlawful killing; autopsy report never given to it → **Oct 2012** Human Rights Watch, *Death of a Dictator* → **Tue 3 Feb 2026** his son Saif al-Islam is shot dead in Zintan.

Compiled 30 Oct 2026. The 15th anniversary was Tue 20 Oct 2026; the 16th is Wed 20 Oct 2027 [COMPUTED: CAL].

## How to read this file

Every fact carries a status tag and one or more source IDs (section 0).

- **[RECORD]**: primary/official: UN reports, NATO statements, US government texts (White House, State, DoD/AFPS, VOA, DVIDS), the OAU/AU's own documents, the UN verbatim record, ICC records; and **Human Rights Watch's on-site findings** (counts it made itself, interviews it conducted; its conclusions are tagged REPORTED where they are inference).
- **[REPORTED]**: contemporaneous wires, newspapers and broadcasters (AP, AFP, Reuters, BBC, NYT, PBS, Channel 4, The East African, Euronews), and later reporting.
- **[CLAIM]**: one person's or one side's assertion, unverified (the Benghazi fighter, the "French agent", Putin, the NTC's "crossfire"). Never narrate as fact.
- **[DISPUTED]**: sources disagree (each version given).
- **[COMPUTED]**: our arithmetic (weekdays, time zones, sun, intervals, distances, unit conversions). Method shown.
- **[UNKNOWN]**: the record we read is silent; what would settle it is named.
- **(fetch)** = read through a page-summary tool, not word for word. **(snippet)** = search-result snippet only. **(repro)** = read in a full reproduction on another site (Atlantic Council NATOSource, Scoop, a BBC mirror). **WIKI** = Wikipedia, lead-finder only.

Rules kept: no invented numbers, quotes or sources; UNKNOWN where the record is silent. Long quotes only from US government text (public domain). UN, NATO, OAU/AU, HRW texts: short quotes, attributed. Libyan and Arabic state texts and all modern press: short quotes or paraphrase, attributed (see 14).

**Content flags for the edit (hard rules):**
- **The capture and the death were filmed and are extremely graphic. No frame of any capture, ambulance, morgue or meat-locker footage or photo, ever. No body, ever** (his, Mutassim's, Abu Bakr Younis's, or the convoy dead).
- **The death: one plain line.** "Within hours he was dead." **The abuse: one plain line.** "Human Rights Watch says he was beaten and abused; within the hour he was dead" is the owner's line; the record does not fix the minute of death, so the safest form is in section 11 ("Human Rights Watch and the UN say he was beaten and abused after his capture. By the time the ambulance reached Misrata, he was dead.").
- **DO NOT NARRATE (note for the editor only):** HRW chapter IV, HRW's 16/17 Oct 2012 news release and UN Annex I describe specific wounds and specific abuse during the capture, and the state of the bodies. None of it is narrated, paraphrased, captioned or shown. This file deliberately leaves the detail out.
- **The Mahari Hotel killings (dozens of prisoners shot):** one line maximum, numbers only, no description of the bodies.
- **No mockery.** Do not use "caught like a rat", no comic music or sound effects at the pipes, no "karma" framing. Plain, documentary tone.
- **Women and girls: no images, ever.** Hillary Clinton, Aisha Gaddafi, his wife, any female bodyguards, the women and children in the convoy, women reporters and officials: narration only (3.2).
- **Children:** the UN says women and children were in the convoy; narrate only, never imply any child is shown.
- **Drone/aircraft attribution:** "NATO aircraft" is the record; "a US Predator drone" is **reported** (unnamed US officials); "a French Mirage" is the French defence minister's statement. Never "the US killed Gaddafi".

## 8. Known vs unknown (as of October 2026)

**Known (record):** On Thursday, October 20, 2011, a convoy of about fifty vehicles carrying Muammar Gaddafi broke out of Sirte at 8 a.m.; at about 8:30 NATO aircraft struck it; he and about a dozen men fled on foot to drainage pipes under a road; a bodyguard's grenade killed his defence minister and wounded him; about 11 a.m. Misrata fighters took him alive; he was beaten and abused, put in an ambulance, and was dead when it reached Misrata; his son Mutassim, also captured alive, was dead by the afternoon; both bodies were shown in a Misrata meat locker for three days and buried in secret on October 25. [RECORD: NATO211021; HRW12; UN-COI-ANX]

**Not known (and what would settle it):**
- **Who fired the shot that killed him, and when he died.** Settle: the autopsy report (never given to the UN) and an independent investigation; the full uncut phone videos.
- **The cause of death.** UN pathologist: inconclusive from photographs; the temple wound may be shrapnel.
- **Who killed Mutassim.** UN: "unable to obtain any account".
- **Which aircraft fired first, and on whose orders.** NATO will not name nations; US involvement rests on unnamed officials. Settle: declassified NATO/US strike logs (none published that we found).
- **The clock of the second strike**, and which nation's jets dropped the GBU-12s.
- **What NATO and allied intelligence knew before 8:30 a.m.** (a *Der Spiegel* report of an intercepted satellite call is a WIKI lead only, not read).
- **The burial site.** Never disclosed.
- **The exact number in the convoy and how many died in the strike vs after capture.**
- **His exact words.** Five versions (4.8 #12).
- **Whether any investigation reached a finding.** Misrata said "nearly finished" in March 2012; nothing published that we found.
- **Whether anyone was ever charged** with his killing or the Mahari executions: no charge found in the sources read.

**The payoff line:** "In 2008, two hundred kings called him the king of kings of Africa. In 2011, NATO stopped his convoy without knowing he was in it, his own guard's grenade wounded him beside a drainage pipe, and the men from Misrata took him alive. Fifteen years later, no court has said who killed him, and no one has said where he is buried."
