# PAN AM 103: THE LAST HOUR OF "CLIPPER MAID OF THE SEAS" (verified research). Wednesday 21 December 1988, all times GMT/UTC (= UK local time). **18:04** pushback from stand Kilo 14 at London Heathrow. **18:25** takeoff from runway 27R. **18:56** level at 31,000 ft (FL310). **18:58** first contact with Shanwick Oceanic. **19:02:44** Shanwick begins reading the oceanic clearance. **19:02:50 ±1 s** a "sudden loud sound" on the cockpit voice recorder, then the recording stops. **19:03** a police patrol in Lockerbie radios that an aircraft has crashed. **20:25** the nose section is found at Tundergarth, about 4 km (2.5 mi) east of the town. After that: the investigation (timer fragment, Toshiba radio-cassette, the suitcase from Malta), the **Camp Zeist verdict of Wed 31 Jan 2001**, the appeals, the **2007 and 2020 SCCRC referrals**, the **2020 US charges against Abu Agila Mas'ud** (accused, pleaded not guilty), and his US federal trial, with **jury selection set for Tue 19 Jan 2027**.

Channel: The Last Hours (@TheLastHoursDocs). Audience: US, Canada and UK, aged 35+, mostly watching on TV. Format: an hour-by-hour countdown, told only from the record and narrated by the File Keeper. Folder: 21_pan_am_103. Length: about 10-12 min. Release: **Mon 21 Dec 2026, the 38th anniversary** (13,879 days after 21 Dec 1988) [COMPUTED: CAL]. The release falls **29 days before jury selection** in Washington, DC [COMPUTED: CAL]. That makes this a **live criminal case**: see section 11 (legal).

Spine: **Mon 5 Dec 1988** an anonymous caller to the US Embassy in Helsinki warns that a Pan Am flight from Frankfurt will be bombed → **Wed 7 Dec** (the date the court found) clothing is bought at Mary's House, Sliema, Malta → **Tue 20 Dec** Megrahi enters Malta on a passport in the name "Abdusamad" (the court found) → **Wed 21 Dec, morning** Air Malta KM180 (check-in 08:15-09:15 Malta time) to Frankfurt → **16:53 Frankfurt time** the Pan Am 727 feeder flight PA103A leaves for London → **18:04-19:02:50 GMT** the last hour → **19:03-20:25** Lockerbie: Sherwood Crescent, Rosebank Crescent, Tundergarth → **Fri 13 Jan 1989** police find charred cloth (later item PI/995) near Newcastleton → **Fri 12 May 1989** Dr Thomas Hayes extracts the fragment later called PT/35(b) → **Mon 6 Aug 1990** AAIB report submitted → **June 1990** the fragment is matched to an MST-13 timer at FBI headquarters → **Thu 14 Nov 1991** US and Scottish charges against Abdelbaset al-Megrahi and Lamin Khalifa Fhimah → **Mon 5 Apr 1999** handover → **Wed 3 May 2000** trial opens at Camp Zeist, Netherlands → **Wed 31 Jan 2001** Megrahi guilty, Fhimah not guilty → **Thu 14 Mar 2002** appeal refused → **Fri 15 Aug 2003** Libya's letter to the UN → **Thu 28 Jun 2007** SCCRC refers the case back → **Thu 20 Aug 2009** compassionate release → **Sun 20 May 2012** Megrahi dies in Tripoli → **Wed 11 Mar 2020** second SCCRC referral → **Mon 21 Dec 2020** US criminal complaint against Mas'ud → **Fri 15 Jan 2021** posthumous appeal refused → **Thu 14 Jul 2022** UK Supreme Court refuses permission → **Sun 11 Dec 2022** US confirms Mas'ud is in custody → **Feb 2023** he pleads not guilty → **Mon 24 Aug 2026** trial postponed days before jury selection → **Mon 5 Oct 2026** new date set → **Thu 15 Oct 2026** copy of his alleged 2012 statement ruled admissible → **Tue 19 Jan 2027** jury selection.

**Clock note (read first):**
- **The UK was on GMT (UTC+0) in December 1988**, with no summer time. The AAIB report states "All times in this report are UTC" (AAIB90 p.1). So every AAIB time is also UK local time.
- **New York, Washington and Toronto were on EST (UTC-5): UK time minus 5 hours.** Los Angeles: minus 8. Frankfurt and Malta (CET, UTC+1): plus 1 [COMPUTED: TZ].
  - Pushback 18:04 GMT = **1:04 p.m. EST**.
  - Takeoff 18:25 GMT = **1:25 p.m. EST** (10:25 a.m. in Los Angeles).
  - Level at 31,000 ft 18:56 GMT = 1:56 p.m. EST.
  - **The loud sound, 19:02:50 GMT = 2:02:50 p.m. EST**, Wednesday afternoon in New York (11:02 a.m. in Los Angeles; 8:02 p.m. in Frankfurt and Malta).
  - Nose section found 20:25 GMT = 3:25 p.m. EST.
  - **In America it was early Wednesday afternoon; the flight had been due in New York that evening.** (The scheduled JFK arrival time is UNKNOWN: it is not in the record read.)
- **The sky (COMPUTED, story beat).** At Lockerbie (55.121°N, 3.354°W) on 21 Dec 1988:
  - Sunrise 08:38, sunset **15:46**, end of astronomical twilight 18:04 GMT. At Heathrow: sunset 15:55, full dark 17:59. **The flight took off in full darkness and was hit 3 h 17 min after sunset over Lockerbie** (NOAA solar algorithm).
  - **The December solstice fell at about 15:28 UTC that same afternoon** (Meeus algorithm, ±2 min). The night of 21/22 December 1988 was the longest night of the year in the Northern Hemisphere. TIME called it "the first night of winter" (TIME890102).
  - **The moon was about 95% lit (waxing gibbous; full on 23 Dec, approx.).** But the AAIB weather aftercast gives "4 to 6 oktas of stratocumulus based at 2,200 feet", "intermittent rain or showers", and visibility "over 15 kilometers" (AAIB90 p.12). **Do not narrate moonlight.** Say "a wet, windy winter night".
- **Winds (AAIB90 p.12):** a weakening jet stream of "around 115 knots above Flight Level 310"; wind at FL320 from 260° at 115 kt; at the surface from 240° at 15-20 kt, gusting 25-30. This west wind is why the debris trails run east, as far as the North Sea.
- Unless marked, every clock time in this file is GMT/UTC.

## How to read this file

Every fact carries a status tag and a source ID (section 0).
- **[RECORD]:** official or primary. AAIB report; court opinions; DOJ, CIA and State Department texts; the Garden of Remembrance plaque; judge's words as reported verbatim by PA from court.
- **[REPORTED]:** wire services, newspapers, Wikipedia used as a lead-finder.
- **[CLAIM]:** one side's assertion. Prosecutors' allegations against Mas'ud, defence positions, Libya's statements, Megrahi's protestations.
- **[DISPUTED]:** sources or parties disagree; each version is given.
- **[COMPUTED]:** our arithmetic (weekdays, time zones, sun and moon, distances, intervals). The method is shown.
- **[UNKNOWN]:** the record we read is silent; what would settle it is named.
- **(OCR)** = read through our OCR of a scanned PDF (wording checked against the page image for every quote used). **(fetch)** = read through a page-summary tool. **(snippet)** = search-result snippet only.

Rules kept: no invented numbers, quotes or sources; UNKNOWN where the record is silent. Long quotes only from US government text (public domain) or Crown/OGL text. Press and books: short quotes or paraphrase, attributed.

**Content flags for the edit (hard rules):**
- **270 died: 259 aboard (243 passengers, 16 crew) and 11 on the ground** [RECORD: AAIB90 p.1, p.4]. **No victim names, no victim photos, no bodies, no remains, no hospital or morgue imagery, ever.** That includes the crew: the AAIB gives their ages and flying hours but no names; do not add names.

- **Fhimah was acquitted.** The 2020 US affidavit names him in its allegations: if used, say "the US complaint alleges", and add every time that a Scottish court acquitted him in 2001.
- **Women and girls: no images, ever.** Narration only for: Judge Dabney L. Friedrich, Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher, Ann McLaughlin (chair of the President's Commission), US officials at memorial events (Loretta Lynch, Lisa Monaco), the defence lawyer who entered the plea, and any flight attendant or passenger.

## 6. What the record shows, and what it doesn't

**The record shows (RECORD):**
1. The clock: pushback 18:04, takeoff 18:25, level 18:56, Shanwick 18:58, clearance 19:02:44, loud sound 19:02:50 ±1 s (AAIB).
2. A bomb, not a mechanical failure: plastic explosive in container AVE 4041 at station 700; no defect, no pre-existing fatigue exploited; 2-3 seconds to lose the nose; no time for the crew to react (AAIB pp.56-57, p.17).
3. Where it fell: the wing in Sherwood Crescent (crater 155 ft), the rear fuselage in Rosebank Crescent, the nose at Tundergarth (found 20:25), debris 81 miles to the North Sea (AAIB).
4. The bomb's packaging as the court accepted it: a Toshiba RT-SF16 inside a brown Samsonite, wrapped in clothes from a shop in Sliema; an MST-13 timer of the single-solder-mask type (HCJ01 [9]-[15], [82]).
5. That three judges convicted Megrahi and acquitted Fhimah; two appeal benches (2002, 2021) upheld the conviction; the UK Supreme Court refused to hear more (2022).
6. That a warning about a Pan Am flight from Frankfurt reached US embassies 16 days before (CIA, TIME).

**The record does NOT show (UNKNOWN / DISPUTED):**
1. **How the suitcase got onto KM180 at Luqa.** The court said so itself ("a major difficulty"), and the Malta baggage records balanced (55 loaded, 55 on the plan).
2. **That the shop sale was on 7 Dec**, not 23 Nov (DISPUTED: weather and Christmas-lights evidence; the SCCRC in 2007 questioned the date finding, as reported).
3. **That the Helsinki warning had anything to do with this bomb.** No link was shown; the Finnish government said it knew the caller and did not take it seriously (TIME).
4. **That the early claims were true.** The CIA's 22 Dec list ("Guardians of the Islamic Revolution", "Islamic Jihad", others) was a first-morning analysis; the court found "no evidence" that the PFLP-GC or PPSF were involved (HCJ01 [82]).
5. **That Mas'ud did anything at all.** These are allegations resting largely on a contested 2012 statement taken in Libya after Gaddafi's fall; a copy (not the original) will be used; the jury has not heard a word yet.
6. **What the newly disclosed evidence of August 2026 is** (one new witness; contents not public).
7. **The crew's last words**: not published. The CVR was noisy and incompletely erased (AAIB p.14).
8. **The exact scheduled arrival in New York, and PA103A's landing time at Heathrow**: not in the records read.

## 10. Sensitivity notes

UK relatives are divided on Megrahi; many US relatives back the Mas'ud prosecution. Our film goes out on the anniversary itself: **no shock edits on the impact, no sound-design "boom" over Lockerbie footage, no music under the dedication.**
