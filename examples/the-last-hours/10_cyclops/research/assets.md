## Best 5 for the thumbnail (one strong real image filling the frame + 2 short text lines)
1. **B1, Harris & Ewing glass negative "U.S.S. Cyclops"** (Library of Congress, PD, 10251×7520): the whole ship broadside, coaling towers like a skeleton against a pale sky, dark water. Grade cold teal. Text: "306 GONE" / "LAST WORD: COAL". No people readable.
2. **C1, Navy Department photo card: Gordon Dewees, Seaman 2c, "Lost on U.S.S. Cyclops June 14, 1918"** (NARA 111-SC-43720, PD, 8554×5749): a young sailor's face (a man) beside a typed card. Crop to the face + placard. Text: "306 LOST" / "NO TRACE". A real face lifts CTR.
3. **A5, Daniels 1922, p. 245: the text of the last message** (PD, 1965×3305): crop tight to "Arrived Barbados, West Indies, 17303 for bunker coal. Arrive Baltimore, Md., 12013." Text-as-image thumbnail. Text: "THE LAST MESSAGE".
4. **B2, NH 55549, Cyclops at anchor in the Hudson, 3 Oct 1911** (NHHC, PD; TIFF master 5394×3598 in I1): calm water, the ship small and long. With our red line Barbados → Virginia from F2. Text: "542 FT" / "NEVER FOUND".
5. **A1, Worley's 1917 Navy medical form** (OMPF p. 17, PD): crop "Where born, San Francisco, Calif." Text: "HIS SECRET" / "306 GONE". Owner decision (research.md §24): fair to the record, but it implies more than the record proves; use only with the video's careful line.

**Crew-portrait verdict:** four public-domain portraits of men lost aboard exist (Dewees, Ashbee, Crammer, Merriam). **No verified public-domain solo photo of Worley**: the well-known image shows him **with his daughter** (excluded), and the tiny Commons portrait has no source (H1).

## Hard rules applied (everything in A-F passed them)
- **No women or girls.** Every A-F image was opened and checked. People in A-F: Gordon Dewees and Chas. S. Ashbee (C1, C2; young men in sailor uniform); Earnest R. Crammer and Roy S. Merriam (C3, C4; men); sailors in work clothes on the battleship's deck in B6 (men, small); possible small figures on deck in B1, B3, B5 and D-ships (crew; a few pixels high, unreadable). Documents A1-A7 contain only forms, typed text, a map and pencil curves. **Women named in the story** (section 3.4 of research.md) get **narration only**.
- **Excluded for people:** Worley with his daughter (USNI 2020 / Hoehling / NHHC); ship-launch crowds (Proteus 1912: H4 must be checked; Nereus 1913); 1918 newspaper pages with fashion ads (H5); Daniels's book plates of "Yeomen (F)" and the page "Women in the Navy" (pp. 328-329); the U-151 plate taken from a passenger steamer with passengers in the foreground (H8).
- **Real only.** Period photos, Navy documents, real satellite images and real charts. **Excluded:** the 1918 newspaper drawing of a squid attacking a ship (inside H7: crop it out), Cussler novel covers, *The Triangle* (2005) and game/cartoon stills, documentary stills, AI images, fan "wreck" renders.
- **Truth.** All Cyclops photos are **1911-1917** (none from the last voyage): label "Cyclops in 1911" etc. Sister ships are **labelled as sisters** (Jupiter, Neptune, Jason are not Cyclops). The 1929 storm map is a **later reconstruction** ("prepared ... from reports of vessels on March 10, 1918"), not a 1918 forecast. The 1968 wreck has **no photo**.

## Access notes
- **NHHC (history.navy.mil):** blocks plain scripts; works with a normal browser user agent. Photo pages offer `/bin/imageDownload?image=...&rendition=` `cq5dam.thumbnail.319.319.png`, `cq5dam.web.1280.1280.jpeg`, `original` (TIFF). The built-in browser pane could not open history.navy.mil directly this session (images still loaded when embedded).
- **NARA catalog:** the JSON API answers at `https://catalog.archives.gov/proxy/records/search?naId_is=NNN` (multi-word `q=` searches returned the web app instead). Page images download as single JPGs.
- **Wikimedia Commons:** the API rate-limits bursts (HTTP 429); space requests out. `Special:FilePath/FILE?width=N` gives a resized copy.
- **archive.org page images:** `https://archive.org/download/<item>/page/nNNN.jpg` redirects to a BookReader image of the stated size (same URL = same image).
- **Pennsylvania Newspaper Archive (Open ONI):** IIIF images at `https://panewsarchive.psu.edu/iiif/<jp2 path>/full/<width>,/0/default.jpg`; same LCCN pages as Chronicling America (which blocked access with a bot check).
- **NASA Worldview:** snapshot URLs are generated images (same URL = same picture). Boxes are 16:9 and widened by 1/cos(latitude) so the picture isn't stretched.
- **NOAA chart display:** ArcGIS export of the NOAA Chart Display Service (U.S. waters only: no Barbados chart). PNG. Not for navigation (fine for video). Both exports have a **black no-data block on the left/lower-left: crop**.
