# The Last Hours, file 09: USS Cyclops (lost March 1918): credits

## Narration
The creator's own voice, cloned locally (Chatterbox Turbo, MIT). Script: `vo_lines.txt`; every fact from `research/research.md` (sections 16-17 checked).
Quotes are from U.S. government text (the Navy's cable, telegram and forms, the Barbados consul's cable as transcribed, the Robison report on Kiel)
or short attributed lines (Nervig's memoir, the consul's son, Dean Hawes). "306" is always given as the Navy's count; records range 293-312.
