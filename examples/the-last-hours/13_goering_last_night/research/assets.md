# Göring's last night (Nuremberg, Tuesday 15 October 1946): visuals DOWNLOAD LIST (nothing downloaded)

Compiled 29 Oct 2026 for **The Last Hours**, folder 13_goering_last_night. **Nothing has been downloaded to disk.** The owner approves every item. Every image was opened in the in-app browser to check it by eye (contact sheets at 128-395 px wide; the key frames and every multi-person frame at 260-790 px). Video was checked with seek points in the browser.
- **px** is the pixel size of that exact URL. I read it from the image header (a 256 KB range read held in memory, never saved), the Commons API or ffprobe on the remote file.
- **size** is the byte count of that exact URL (HTTP Content-Range/Length, the Commons API or the NARA catalog). MB = 1,048,576 bytes.
- The **licence** cell links to the source page. Check it before downloading.
- **check** = one-line content check (women / bodies / symbols) and who checked it. "me" = I eyeballed it myself in this pass. There was no assistant pass this time.

## Licence key
- **PD, US federal government work:** US Army Signal Corps / Office of U.S. Chief of Counsel photos (NARA RG 238, series 238-NT "Photographs relating to Major Nuremberg Trials", NAID 540122, all marked **"Unrestricted"**); US Army Board of Proceedings and Andrus memos (NARA RG 242, NAID 7491747, "Unrestricted"); USAAF photos; CIA map; United News (OWI) newsreel 208-UN.
- **IMT "Blue Series"** (Trial of the Major War Criminals, published by the Tribunal's Secretariat, Nuremberg 1947): an official court record, treated as PD. The scans are by Boston Public Library on the Internet Archive, with no rights claim.
- **Göring's own words** (his letters, his note): he died in 1946, so his unpublished writings have been PD in the US (life + 70) and in Germany since 2017.
- **1946 US newspapers:** PD unless the copyright was renewed in 1973-74. Renewals of daily papers were rare. **I did not check the renewal records (CCE).** See H.
- **CC BY-SA** (Commons): credit the author. Keep it out of thumbnails.
- **Pexels licence:** free use, credit optional (we credit). Stand-ins only: label "illustrative".

## Access notes
- **NARA files** load directly. Stills are at `https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/stillpix/238-nt/...` and documents at `https://catalog.archives.gov/medialive/...`. The `s3.amazonaws.com/NARAprodstorage/...` form returns 403, so use the catalog.archives.gov form. The catalog page is `https://catalog.archives.gov/id/<NAID>`.
- **archive.org page images:** `https://archive.org/download/<item>/page/nN.jpg` redirects to a full-size BookReader image (sizes below are for that redirect).
- **loc.gov / Chronicling America** shows a Cloudflare "verify you are human" check in both browsers. It was **not bypassed**, so there are no LoC newspaper pages. The 1946 papers below come from the Internet Archive.
- **maps.lib.utexas.edu (AMS city plans)** sits behind a bot check (not bypassed). The maps come from Commons instead.
- **Pexels** opened in Chrome (no check). Sizes are for the original `images.pexels.com/photos/<id>/pexels-photo-<id>.jpeg`.
- **Commons** rate-limits ffprobe (HTTP 429). Video durations come from the Commons API.
- Helper scripts and metadata text sit in `C:\gx\` (no media). They are safe to delete.
