DESCRIPTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hermann Göring's last night in Nuremberg prison, Tuesday, October 15, 1946, hour by hour from the U.S. Army's own files. A guard at his door 24 hours a day, about 100 searches, and at 10:45 p.m. he was dead of cyanide, about two hours before the hangings began.

You'll see the Army's English copy of the note he wrote four days earlier ("I have always had the capsule of poison with me from the time that I became a prisoner"), the Army board's photo of the case and the glass vial, the prison commandant's memo with the minute each condemned man was brought to the gymnasium, and the four men who later claimed they knew how he did it. None of them has ever been proved right.

An educational documentary built from U.S. Army records, the four-power commission, the Nuremberg trial record and 1946 newspapers. No images of the executions or of the dead are shown.
