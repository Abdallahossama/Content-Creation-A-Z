Göring's Last Note: "I Have Always Had the Capsule" #nuremberg

Four days before he died, Hermann Göring wrote to the prison commandant. The U.S. Army's English copy reads: "I have always had the capsule of poison with me from the time that I became a prisoner." Three capsules, he said: one left in his clothes to be found, one kept on him, one in a round box of skin cream. And no guard was to blame.
