## How to read this file

Every fact carries a status tag and one or more source IDs (section 0).

- **[RECORD]**: primary/official: Egyptian court rulings (the 1955 Cassation ruling, which restates the 1954 trial judgment and the 1953 Revolutionary Court judgment), US government texts (Federal Register, State, Treasury, White House, VOA), the 1988 Hamas covenant, the 2012 Ahram Gate text, contemporary wire copy as printed (AP/INS, read in the newspaper scan).
- **[REPORTED]**: historians (Mitchell, Lia), journalists' reconstructions (Mohsen Mohamed, Heikal), later press (Youm7, Al-Jarida, Ahram Online, Al Jazeera, Bridge Michigan, PolitiFact, AP 2026).
- **[CLAIM]**: one person's or one side's assertion: Banna's memoir, MB narratives (Ikhwan Wiki, the father's account), post-1952 testimony of palace servants, the defence at trial, the Egyptian government's 1949 line, campaign claims in 2026. Never narrate as fact.
- **[DISPUTED]**: sources disagree (each version given).
- **[COMPUTED]**: our arithmetic (weekdays, time zones, sun and moon, Hijri conversions, intervals). Method shown.
- **[UNKNOWN]**: the record we read is silent; what would settle it is named.
- **(fetch)** = read through a page-summary tool. **(snippet)** = search-result snippet only. **(OCR)** = read in a scanned newspaper's OCR text. **(AR)** = Arabic, translated by us; the translation is ours. **WIKI** = Wikipedia, lead-finder only.

Rules kept: no invented numbers, quotes or sources; UNKNOWN where the record is silent. Long quotes only from US government text (public domain). Court rulings: short quotes, translated. Books and press: short quotes or paraphrase, attributed.

**Content flags for the edit (hard rules):**
- **No body, no wound, no blood on screen, ever.** No hospital, morgue or autopsy imagery; no re-enactment of the shooting. The shooting is a sound cue and a dark street, or a document.

- **No judgement words** in narration: not "terrorist", "extremist", "martyr", "murderous regime". Quote words like "terrorist" only inside an attributed quote when the quote is the point (e.g. the 2026 designations) and say who said it.

- **Living people (2026 chapter):** no image of Abdul El-Sayed except a public-domain one cropped to him alone (assets.md J1); every claim attributed; no implication of membership (section 9 and 13).

## 5. The mechanism: how a car number survived a cover-up

1. The gunmen waited near the YMMA "which they knew he was in" (court) → only al-Naghi and al-Laithi knew the hour (defence point; MOHSEN).
2. A boy in a galabiya shouted the getaway car's number; he was never found (court: real).
3. Al-Laithi repeated it to everyone, including the man "from the Hikimdar" (MOHSEN: Lt-Col. Wasfi).
4. *al-Misri* printed it; the censor seized the run; ~5,000 copies leaked (MOHSEN): "a fixed piece of evidence" five years later.
5. A political-police officer pressed al-Laithi to blur it ("9979 or 9997"): convicted (court).
6. After July 1952 the driver confessed (later retracted) that he drove two men from the scene to the **Eden Hotel**, where his colonel waited; the court accepted that part (CASS55).
