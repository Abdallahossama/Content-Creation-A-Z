Only A26 (Farouk) carries an explicit {{PD-1996}}. Re-read the template on the file page before use.

- **CC BY 3.0 BR:** Agência Brasil (A46). Credit line required.
- **Universal Newsreel on archive.org:** item licence "publicdomain" (checked 31 Oct 2026: `creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain/`).
- **Newspapers on archive.org:** **no licence on the items** (checked). A US paper of 1948-49 is PD only if its copyright was not renewed (renewal due 1976-77). Check the Copyright Office renewal records before use; never lift the wire photos (AP/Acme) printed on the pages.

- **Library of Congress (incl. Chronicling America), UT map library (PCL), California Digital Newspaper Collection:** bot challenges; not searched directly. Matson photos and AMS maps were found through their Commons copies.

- **Internet Archive:** newspapers and Universal Newsreels open; page image pattern `https://archive.org/download/<id>/page/n<k>.jpg`.
