# The Last Hours, file 06: EgyptAir Flight 990 (31 October 1999): credits

## Narration
The creator's own voice, cloned locally (Chatterbox, MIT licence). Script: research/research.md (every claim sourced there).
Crew are named by role only, as in the NTSB brief. No cockpit audio has ever been released: transcript lines are shown as text,
typeset by us from the NTSB transcript (AAB-02/01, appendix), and read by the narrator.
