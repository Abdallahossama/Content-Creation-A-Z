"Did You Shut the Engines?" "It's Shut." Then: "Pull With Me." #egyptair

1:50 a.m. EgyptAir 990 is diving toward the Atlantic at almost the speed of sound. On the flight data recorder, the left elevator pulls the nose up while the right one pushes it down. Both engines are switched off. The captain asks the relief first officer why.

The NTSB Said What Crashed EgyptAir 990. It Never Said Why. #egyptair

March 21, 2002. The final report on EgyptAir Flight 990. The probable cause: the relief first officer's flight control inputs. The reason for them was not determined. Egypt's reply is printed in the same document: the evidence is not sufficient to identify one cause.
