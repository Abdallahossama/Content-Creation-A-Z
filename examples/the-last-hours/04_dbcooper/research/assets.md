## Licence key
- **PD (FBI / US gov work)**: works of federal employees are not copyrighted (17 U.S.C. 105). Covers the FBI evidence photos, FBI file pages, USAF, US Army, US Forest Service, USGS and NASA items.
- **PD (EPA DOCUMERICA / NARA)**: EPA's 1971-77 federal photo project, held by the National Archives; public domain.
- **PD (author released it)**: the Cooper-vane photo, released to the public domain by its author on Commons.
- **Public Domain Mark**: the Internet Archive mirror of the FBI file is marked that way (the pages are FBI records anyway).
- **Pexels licence**: free commercial use, attribution optional.
- **CC BY / CC BY-SA, a weak PD claim, and Copernicus data (attribution)**: only in section H, flagged in bold.

## Hard rules applied (everything in A-F passed them)
- **No women or girls.** Every photo was checked by eye on a contact sheet. None of the three flight attendants appears in any image here. The FBI file pages name the stewardesses in text only (no pictures of them). All rejects are listed at the end.
- **Real only.** FBI evidence photos, FBI documents, period (1972-73) government photos, USGS maps and satellite views. No animations, no Wikipedia diagrams, no comic art, no film/TV, no AI. The FBI composite sketches are real evidence.
- **Copyright-safe only.** No AP, Getty, Seattle Times, Seattle P-I, Oregonian or Columbian photos (that rules out all 1980 press photos of the money find and of Brian Ingram).

## Could not find
- **Cooper's note.** No image exists: he took the note back from the flight attendant, and the FBI never recovered it. The file only has typed transcriptions.
- **The FBI's own drawn flight-path charts from 1971.** The airtels say two aeronautical charts with the path drawn "with a green marker pen" were enclosed, but the enclosures are not in the scanned Vault pages. A5 (the chart strip from the FBI's 2007 gallery) is the one that survives online.
- **A PD photo of N467US itself.** The only one (Miami, Dec 1972) is GFDL-only (see Excluded). Sections B and H use other Northwest Orient 727s of the same years.
- **A PD photo of a 727 with the aft airstair lowered.** Only a CC BY 1975 Northwest photo with passengers (excluded) and CC BY animations (not real). B2 shows the belly of a 727 from below instead.
- **PD 1970s photos of Seattle-Tacoma airport or of Reno's airport.** None on Commons or in DOCUMERICA. C6 (Seattle at dusk) and C7 (Reno neon) stand in.
- **A PD photo of Lake Merwin / Ariel today or in the 1970s.** Only CC BY-SA (H4). The 1971 USGS Ariel map (C10) and the 1973 Landsat view (F3) cover the drop zone.
- **A PD photo of Tena Bar or of the Feb 1980 FBI dig.** The FBI's dig photos are not in the scanned file; the 1980 press photos (AP, The Columbian) are copyrighted. Use C11 (topo), A23 (sediment diagram) and E8 (Pexels sand bar).
- **Ralph Himmelsbach, the flight crew on their own, Army/National Guard search photos of 1972, and FAA photos of the vane**: no PD images found.
- **1971 airline cabin interiors with no women in frame**: the only free period cabin slides (SDASM, PSA 727) were not used; period cabin shots almost always show stewardesses.
- **Library of Congress / NARA catalog** were not searched directly (Commons already carries the NARA DOCUMERICA scans).
