On November 24, 1971, a man who called himself Dan Cooper hijacked Northwest Orient Flight 305 with a briefcase he said held a bomb. He got $200,000 and four parachutes, and somewhere over southwest Washington he jumped out of the back of a Boeing 727 into the rain and the dark. The FBI never found out who he was.

This is that night, hour by hour, from the FBI's own files: the $20 ticket, the note, the circling over Seattle, the money and the parachutes, his flight plan, the last words anyone recorded from him at 8:05 PM, the empty seat in Reno, the search near Ariel, the $5,800 a boy found in the sand in 1980, and the new pages and findings from 2025 and 2026.
