## Reconstructions
The hijacker's note and his flight plan are shown as **reconstructions** (labelled on screen). The note was never recovered; the wording follows the flight attendant's account in the FBI file. The flight-plan card follows what the flight attendant wrote down.

## Maps drawn for this video
Natural Earth (public domain) coastlines and US state lines. The flight path is approximate (airway Victor 23 and the FBI's 1971 reconstruction), labelled as approximate on screen.
