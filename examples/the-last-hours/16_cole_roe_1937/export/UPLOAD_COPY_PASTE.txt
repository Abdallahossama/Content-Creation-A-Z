DESCRIPTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Alcatraz, Thursday, December 16, 1937: two convicts counted at 1:00 p.m. were gone by the 1:30 count. Theodore Cole and Ralph Roe cut through the bars of a shop window, forced a wire-fence gate and went into San Francisco Bay in the thickest fog of the winter. They were never found, dead or alive.

You'll see both men's own prison cards, stamped in red "ESCAPED DECEMBER 16, 1937", the 1937 front pages, what NOAA's tide tables say the water was doing that afternoon, why the warden changed his mind three days later, the FBI's 1939 list with Cole at No. 2 and Roe at No. 3, the Tulsa taxi shooting of 1940, and the 1941 "alive in South America" postcard story. Twenty-four years later, three more men left the same island on a raft: that file is on this channel.

An educational documentary built from Bureau of Prisons and National Archives records, National Park Service archives, FBI notices and 1937-1941 newspapers. Tide figures are NOAA's modern predictions run back to 1937 (an estimate).
