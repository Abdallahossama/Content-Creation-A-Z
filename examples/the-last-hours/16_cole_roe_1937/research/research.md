# THE FOG ESCAPE: verified research (Alcatraz Island, Thursday 16 December 1937, between the 1:00 p.m. and 1:30 p.m. counts: Theodore Cole and Ralph Roe leave a workshop in the Model Industries Building, go through the window bars and the wire fence, and are never seen again; the search, the FBI hunt 1937-1941, the 1941 "alive in South America" story, and the official verdict "missing and presumed drowned")

Channel: The Last Hours (@TheLastHoursDocs; US / Canada audience). Format: hour-by-hour countdown with the clock times the record has, told only from the record, narrated by the File Keeper. Folder 16_cole_roe_1937. **Third Alcatraz file** after "What the FBI Found When 3 Men Vanished From Alcatraz" (folder 01_alcatraz, Mon 11 June 1962) and the Battle of Alcatraz (folder 14_battle_of_alcatraz, 2-4 May 1946).

Spine: **Thu 16 Dec 1937**, morning: the thickest fog of the winter on the bay; a spring tide (full moon the next morning) → **1:00 p.m.** count: Cole (AZ-258) and Roe (AZ-260) present → between 1:00 and 1:30 they finish bars they had sawed "a few minutes each day" for months, squeeze through a gap about 8¾ x 18 in, jimmy the gate of a wire fence with a shop wrench, drop about 20 ft to the rocks and enter the water → **~1:40-2:00 p.m.** the computed peak of the outgoing current past Alcatraz (about 3.4-3.7 knots, toward the Golden Gate) → **1:30 p.m.** count: "they were gone" → afternoon and night: Coast Guard, police, soldiers, searchlights and foghorns; caves on the island searched → **Fri 17**: "one chance in a thousand"; the Coast Guard pulls its cutters back → **Sat 18**: the FBI: "We are going to catch everyone connected with this escape"; a "mystery boat" at Richmond → **Mon 20**: the warden changes his mind (a boat "manned by accomplices"); FBI Identification Order 1472 issued for Roe → **Wed 22**: the cutting tool "was not apparently prison property" → 1938-1940: sightings (St. Louis, Pueblo, Tulsa); **Aug 1939** the FBI lists Cole No. 2 and Roe No. 3 among the ten most-sought fugitives → **30 Apr 1941**: the *San Francisco Chronicle*: alive in South America, a postcard code "Business was good in July" → never found. Officially "missing and presumed drowned" (BOP).
Compiled 2026-10-30. 89th anniversary: Wed 16 Dec 2026; **90th: Thu 16 Dec 2027** [COMPUTED: CAL].

**Clock note (read first):** In December 1937 California was on **Pacific Standard Time, UTC-8**. California had no daylight saving between 1919 and the federal War Time of 9 Feb 1942 [REPORTED: TZ, IANA America/Los_Angeles]. So **Alcatraz 1:30 p.m. = 4:30 p.m. New York (EST, UTC-5) = 9:30 p.m. London (GMT)** [COMPUTED: TZ]. Note that file 14 (May 1946) was also PST, while file 01 (June 1962) was PDT. All clock times in this file are Alcatraz local time (PST) as printed by the AP and UP or computed by NOAA in "local standard time".

**Record note:** This event has a thinner official record than 1946 or 1962. What we have: (1) **the Bureau of Prisons' own index cards** for both men (NARA "Warden's Notebook", read in full, both stamped in red "ESCAPED DECEMBER 16, 1937"); (2) **BOP and NPS history pages** (short, and they disagree on details); (3) **the 1937-1941 wire stories** (AP, UP, INS), read as OCR in Utah Digital Newspapers (rights statement "No Copyright - United States") and in Internet Archive newspaper scans; (4) **NOAA harmonic tide and current predictions** recomputed for 16 Dec 1937 (modern constants, see 5); (5) one catalog record of the **FBI Identification Order for Roe**. **No FBI Vault file for the 1937 escape was found** (vault.fbi.gov blocks this connection; archive.org mirrors only hold the 1962 file). Warden Johnston's 1949 book was **not read** (lending copy; full-text search refused). The *San Francisco Chronicle* 1937-1941 coverage was read **only through UP's summaries** and search snippets (the sfgenealogy transcriptions are behind a bot check, which I did not bypass).

## How to read this file

Every fact carries a status tag and one or more source IDs (section 0).

- **[RECORD]**: primary/official: NARA Warden's Notebook cards, BOP and NPS pages, the FBI order catalog record, and statements by Warden Johnston, BOP Director Bennett and FBI officials **as printed** by the wire services.
- **[REPORTED]**: contemporaneous press (AP, UP, INS, the *Chronicle* via UP) and later reporting.
- **[CLAIM]**: one person's claim, a sighting, a rumour or a dramatization. Never narrate as fact.
- **[DISPUTED]**: sources disagree (each version given).
- **[COMPUTED]**: our arithmetic or a model run (tides, currents, sun, moon, distances, weekdays, ages). Method shown.
- **[UNKNOWN]**: the record we read is silent; what would settle it is named.
- **(OCR)** = read in Utah Digital Newspapers OCR (the page scan exists; columns are often mixed, so some quotes are *reconstructed* and marked so). **(snippet)** = search-result text only. **WIKI** = Wikipedia, used only to find leads.

Rules kept: no invented numbers, quotes or sources; "unknown" where the record is silent. Press quotes kept short and attributed.

**Content flags for the edit (hard rules):**
- **No bodies.** None were ever found, so do not show drowning imagery, bodies in water, or "dead man" reconstructions.
- **Cole's prison killing** (his card: "he killed his cell mate by stabbing him with a knife"): one plain line or omit (section 18).
- **Women and girls: no images, ever.** Cole's wife and child (named on nothing we read), the 1939 Seminole witnesses (WIKI): narration only, or leave out.
- **No glorification**: never "legend", "the perfect escape", "outsmarted the Rock". The record says *nobody knows*.

### 4.6 Record problems (pick one version; say "about")

1. **Date:** 16 Dec (cards, BOP, press) vs 17 Dec (WIKI lead). **Use Thursday, December 16th, 1937.**
2. **Which shop:** mat shop (BOP), machine shop (Johnston, AP), blacksmith shop (Roe's card; *Chronicle* 1941), "tire repair shop" (UP photo caption). **Say "a workshop in the Model Industries Building, where convicts cut old tires into mats".**
3. **When they vanished:** "shortly after noon" (AP 17 Dec) vs the 1:00/1:30 counts (Johnston). **Use "between the one o'clock count and the one-thirty count".**
4. **Weather:** heavy fog (all 1937 sources) vs "a bad storm" (BOP today). **Say fog.**
5. **Tide speed:** "seven miles an hour" (AP) / "7-9 knots" (WIKI) vs about 3.4-3.7 knots computed near the island (4.6 at the Golden Gate) from modern NOAA constants; 1937 river floods may have added speed. **Say "one of the strongest outgoing tides of the year", and give the NOAA figure as "about four miles an hour" only if labelled a modern estimate.**
6. **Distance:** "a mile and a quarter" (AP) vs "a mile and a half" (UP). **Say "more than a mile".**
7. **Ages:** Roe 29 in the press, **31** by his card; Cole 23 or 25 in the press, **25** by his card.
8. **Home towns:** Cole "of Stroud" (1937) / "of Guthrie" (1939); Roe "Duncan" (1937) / "of Tulsa" (1939). **Say "Oklahoma".**
9. **Cole's crime:** often called a "bank robber" (WIKI, dealer); his federal sentence was for **kidnapping** (card). His 1929 death sentence was for a robbery.
10. **Arrival:** 26 Oct **1935** (cards) vs "1936" (WIKI).
11. **The alert:** teletype "within a short time" (AP) vs news held back "for hours" (UP). Both can be true (police alert vs press release).

## 8. KNOWN vs THEORY / what nobody knows (as of 30 Oct 2026)

**Known (record):** On Thursday, 16 December 1937, Theodore Cole (AZ-258) and Ralph Roe (AZ-260) were counted at 1:00 p.m. and missing at 1:30 p.m.; they cut through the bars of a shop window in the Model Industries Building, got through a fence gate and went down to the water in heavy fog; they were never found, dead or alive; the FBI issued an identification order for Roe on 20 December 1937 and in August 1939 listed both among its ten most-sought fugitives; the BOP lists them as missing and presumed dead. [RECORD: NARA cards; BOP-HIST; TSU-IO; AP390811-SLTR]

**Theories on the record (each said by someone, none proven):**
1. **Drowned and swept out the Golden Gate** (Johnston 16-18 Dec 1937; Coast Guard; BOP today). Supported by the tide and the cold; weakened by the fact that no body or clothing was ever found.
2. **Picked up by a boat with accomplices** (Johnston, 20 Dec 1937; the Richmond "mystery boat"; the stolen small boat at St. Francis Yacht Harbor). Never proven.
3. **Floated on tire parts or a raft left on the beach** (Johnston's padding theory; the *Chronicle* 1941).
4. **Alive in South America** (the *Chronicle*, 30 Apr 1941, from inmates and a postcard). Never confirmed.

**Not known:** the minute they went into the water; which shop (four versions); where the cutting tool came from (Johnston: not prison property, nothing missing); whether anyone helped; whether the 1941 postcard existed; the FBI's 1937 case file (number and contents; **no Vault release found**); Cole's identification-order number; the water temperature that day; the names of the shop officer and tower guards; whether any warrant is still carried by the U.S. Marshals (not found; the 1962 three are on the USMS site, F01); Johnston's own 1949 account (not read).

**The payoff line:** "Counted at one. Gone at one-thirty. Two files stamped in red, 'Escaped December 16, 1937', and nothing after it."
