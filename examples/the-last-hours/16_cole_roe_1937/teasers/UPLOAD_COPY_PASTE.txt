Counted at 1:00. By 1:30, Two Men Were Gone From Alcatraz. #alcatraz

Alcatraz, Thursday, December 16, 1937, 1:00 p.m. The count is complete; Theodore Cole and Ralph Roe are at work in the shop at the north end of the island. By the 1:30 count they're gone: through a window with the bars cut, over a wire fence, and into the thickest fog of the winter. The guards in the towers can't see them.
