# File 16: the fog escape, Alcatraz (Thursday 16 December 1937): credits

Real images only. No drowning imagery, no bodies (none was ever found); no women on screen: every newspaper page is cropped to the
article (ads, cartoons and photos of women cut away), and the Pexels fog timelapse is cropped to the sky and the bridge tower.
Only the items below appear in the edit (render_coleroe.py SHOTS).
