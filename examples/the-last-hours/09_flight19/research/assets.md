## Best 5 for the thumbnail (one strong real image filling the frame + 2 short text lines)
1. **B1, five TBF-1 Avengers in echelon off Fort Lauderdale, January 1943** (U.S. Navy / NARA, PD, 3000×2445): five Avengers stepped back over a flat grey sea, the same station and the same airplane family as Flight 19. Grade it cold teal at dusk. Text: "14 MEN" / "NO ANSWER". Label in the description: "1943 Navy photo, not the Flight 19 aircraft". (B2 is the alternate frame from the same shoot.)
2. **C1, Lt. Charles C. Taylor's student-aviator photo, 8 Aug 1941** (NHHC NH 84038; high-res TIFF 5028×4838): a young man's face with the placard "STUDENT AVIATOR U.S.N.R. ... TAYLOR C.C.". A real face (a man) lifts CTR. Text: "7:04 PM" / "LAST CALL". Owner decision (research.md §24): sensitive because the Navy first blamed him.
3. **A13, the Rough Crash Log** (NAS Fort Lauderdale, PD): crop tight to "1904 — Last transmissions from the flight (T-3 calling T-28) no answer". Text-as-image thumbnail; 2744×4344 frame, the line itself is about 2000 px wide.
4. **A4, "Navigation Problem No. 1" chart** (NARA 73985447, PD, 2978×2437): the actual training-route chart with the triangle drawn over the Bahamas. Add our red circle at 29°N 79°W (the 5:50 fix). Text: "5 PLANES" / "LOST HERE".
5. **B6, a PBM-5 Mariner in flight, c. 1945** (U.S. Navy, PD, 1875×1287): the same type as Training 49. For title option 3. Text: "13 MORE MEN" / "100-FT FLAMES".

**Crew-portrait verdict:** only Lt. Taylor has a clearly public (Navy/NHHC) photo. All other crew photos found online come from the NAS Fort Lauderdale Museum (marked ©) or family collections: none listed. For the others use names on screen over a dimmed sea or the accident cards.

## Hard rules applied (everything in A-F passed them)
- **No women or girls.** Every image was opened and checked. People in A-F: Lt. Taylor (C1, a man); two sailors in uniform standing by the hull in B7 (men); shirtless men of the beaching crew in the water in B8 (men); two men hosing the hull in B9 (men); tiny unreadable crew on the decks of USS Solomons (D1-D3: lines of figures a few pixels high, no faces). Documents A1-A33 contain only text, signatures and stamps. **Women named in the documents** (none on these pages) and in the story (Katherine Taylor, Mary Carroll, Georgia Lowe) get **narration only**.
- **Excluded for people:** the 11 Apr 2017 Banana River monument ceremony photo (Commons "They don't ask why, they only ask where", USAF, PD): a saluting audience, women likely; the Smithsonian's lead photo of Walter Parpart with another person (also not ours); NAS Fort Lauderdale Museum group photos.
- **Real only.** Period photos, Navy documents, real satellite images and real charts. **Excluded:** fan-made renders and maps on Commons (Anynobody "Tbf-19-5.png", "Tbf-3c.png", "Ft28animation.gif"; Merikanto "Flight 19 vector map" SVGs and "raster map" PNGs, which add their own guesses), *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* stills, documentary stills, AI images.
- **Truth.** Archival Avengers and Mariners show the **same types**, not the Flight 19 or Training 49 aircraft: label them. The FT-28 warbird (H1-H2) is a **replica paint scheme** on a different airframe (BuNo 91188). The Navigation Problem chart (A4) is the Board's exhibit of the *planned* route, not the flight's actual track.

## Access notes
- **NARA catalog:** the web catalog is a script app; the JSON behind it answers at `https://catalog.archives.gov/proxy/records/search?naId_is=146850136` (lists all 550 objects). Single frames download fast (0.4-0.9 MB each) and are upright even where the PDF page is rotated (frames 295, 299, 302, 308, 545).
- **NHHC (history.navy.mil):** blocks plain scripts; works with a normal browser user agent. Photo pages offer three renditions through `/bin/imageDownload?image=...&rendition=` : `cq5dam.thumbnail.319.319.png`, `cq5dam.web.1280.1280.jpeg`, `original` (TIFF, 23-31 MB).
- **Wikimedia Commons:** download one at a time, a few seconds apart, with a plain user agent (429 on bursts).
- **NASA Worldview:** snapshot URLs are generated images (same URL = same picture). Boxes are set so the picture isn't stretched at 25-30°N. Terra/Aqua MODIS have black orbit gaps on some dates; the chosen dates have none in frame.
- **NOAA chart display:** ArcGIS export of the NOAA Chart Display Service (covers the Bahamas too). PNG, 0.6-1.5 MB. Not for navigation (fine for video).
- **Pexels:** CDN URLs at w=1920 returned 200 in the EgyptAir check (22 Oct 2026); the site shows a bot check to scripts.
