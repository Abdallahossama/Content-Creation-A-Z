# The Last Hours, file 08: Flight 19 and Training 49 (5 December 1945): credits

## Narration
The creator's own voice, cloned locally (Chatterbox Turbo, MIT). Script: `vo_lines.txt`; every fact from `research/research.md` (sections 16-17 checked).
No audio of Flight 19 exists. Quotes are read from the 1945 Navy radio logs and Lt. Robert F. Cox's sworn testimony (U.S. government works, public domain).
