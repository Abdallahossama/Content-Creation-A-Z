Flight 19, December 5, 1945, told from the Navy's own radio logs. Five Avenger torpedo bombers and 14 men took off from Fort Lauderdale on a training flight and were never found. The last line in the crash log, at 7:04 p.m.: "T-3 calling T-28. No answer."

This file follows them hour by hour from the record: the forecast nobody mentions, "Both my compasses are out", "Don't come after me" (real, and later rewritten), the student who said fly west, the ready plane that stayed on the ground, the 5:50 p.m. position the Navy never broadcast, the turn east, Training 49 and the 100-foot flames, the five-day search, the verdict a mother fought, and how the Bermuda Triangle legend was built on last words nobody said.
