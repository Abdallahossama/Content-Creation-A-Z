September 2009, the United Nations in New York. Gaddafi was given fifteen minutes. He spoke for more than an hour and a half, tore the corner of the UN Charter, and called the Security Council the Terror Council. The world saw a showman. June 11, 1984. Late at night, Gaddafi speaks live on Libyan radio about the men who attacked his barracks: "This dog was trained in America." Then a line meant for Washington. Twenty-five years later, he stood in New York and called the Security Council the Terror Council.

Thousands of Students Were Bussed In to Watch a "Trial" #gaddafi

Tuesday, June 5, 1984. Benghazi. The sixth day of Ramadan. Thousands of schoolchildren and students are bussed into the city's basketball stadium. On the court, a young engineer kneels, hands bound. Two young men run out of the crowd and beg for mercy. It didn't matter.
