## THE 1984 BROADCAST: NOT IN THIS LIST (verdict: none)
The Libyan state TV film of the "trial" and hanging in Benghazi's basketball stadium (recovered in 2011 from four Beta tapes; edited versions published by the Guardian and Al Jazeera, July 2011) is **excluded, and so are stills, frame grabs, blurred versions and its audio**. Libya has been a Berne country since 1976 (Circular 38a); the film is very likely protected in the US either as an unpublished work (to about 2104) or as a restored work (to about 2079); publishers' copies can trigger Content ID; and it shows a real execution, which our rules and YouTube's graphic-violence policy forbid. Full reasoning: research.md section 18. **We tell the stadium with the radio's own words (A13), US government pages, Gaddafi's face and texture, never with its pictures.**
