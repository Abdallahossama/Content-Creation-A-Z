# Alcatraz 1962: candidate visuals (NOT downloaded, for approval)

Compiled 2026-10-19. Nothing has been downloaded. All URLs are for the owner to review and approve.
Beat codes refer to research.md: PREP (months before), 930 (lights out), ROOF (roof/stack), 1000 (shore/launch), 1030 (noise), TIDE (tide/night water), 0715 (discovery), HUNT (manhunt/evidence), FATE (case outcome and later claims), TODAY (still wanted), MAP.

## Licence key
- **PD-USGov**: work of a US federal employee (FBI, BOP, USMS, NPS, USGS, NOAA, LOC/HABS). Public domain in the US. Credit the agency anyway (put it in CREDITS.md).
- **NPS-PD**: NPGallery marks the item "Public domain: Full Granting Rights".
- **Pexels**: Pexels License (free commercial use, no attribution required, no resale as-is). **Pixabay**: Pixabay Content License (similar).
- **CC BY / CC BY-SA**: free with credit (and share-alike for SA). Optional only, flagged.

## Hard exclusions (do not use)
- Anything from the 1979 film "Escape from Alcatraz", "Loki", "Unsolved Mysteries", MythBusters, Nat Geo, the HISTORY 2015 special (including the Brizzi "Brazil" photo, which is family/HISTORY copyright), the Mark Rober/Johnny Harris video, or news broadcasts.
- **Modern tourist photos of the "escape cells" with heads in the beds.** Until Nov 2018 the heads shown in those cells were **props from the 1979 film** (AP, 15 Nov 2018). The replicas shown since then are FBI 3D prints. That rules out Commons "Escape Alcatraz Cell.jpg", "Alcatraz Cells 7–10.JPG", "Escape From Alcatraz.jpg", "Alcatraz Cell.jpg" and similar.
- Commons "Old Frank Morris.png", "Old John Anglin.png", "Old Clarence Anglin.jpg": fan-made age progressions (CC BY-SA "own work"), not official.
- Commons "Allen West (criminal).jpg": "own work" claim with unclear provenance. **No PD photo of Allen West was found.** Show his cell/vent instead.
- NPGallery items in the same album that show women or girls: "Alcatraz women's cookbook", "Alcatraz Girl's Club", "Young adults… Christmas Party", "men and women unloading…", "Four children playing…". Excluded by the channel rule.
- Pexels/Pixabay clips with women, or with staged actors in modern orange jumpsuits (RDNE "Woman inside the Jail", "A Woman in Jail", "Prisoner Reading a Book", etc.).

## Access note
fbi.gov, vault.fbi.gov and USMS PDF links return 403 ("request blocked") from this connection (it looks geo/IP-based). Workarounds:
- FBI images: try the Wayback form `https://web.archive.org/web/2026im_/<fbi image URL>`. The famous-cases page itself is archived (snapshot 2026-04-28), but each image's archived copy is **not individually verified**.
- FBI Vault PDFs: use the full mirror on archive.org (same documents): https://archive.org/details/Alcatraz-FBI (each part also comes as `alcatrN_text.pdf`, which is searchable).
