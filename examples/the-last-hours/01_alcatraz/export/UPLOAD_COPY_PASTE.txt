On the night of June 11, 1962, Frank Morris and the brothers John and Clarence Anglin climbed out of their cells on Alcatraz and were never seen again. This is the escape told hour by hour, from the FBI's own file: the dummy heads, the vacuum-cleaner drill, the raincoat raft, the noise a guard heard at 10:30, the tide that night, the manhunt, and the letter that arrived 51 years later.
