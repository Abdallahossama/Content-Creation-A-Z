# NICOLAE CEAUȘESCU'S LAST DAYS: verified research (Bucharest and Târgoviște, Thursday 21 December 1989, about 12:30 p.m.: the balcony rally that turns into boos; the night of 21-22 December: shooting at University Square; Friday 22 December, 12:06-12:09 p.m.: the helicopter lifts off the Central Committee roof; Snagov, the landing near Boteni about 1:30 p.m., two commandeered Dacias, the Plant Protection Centre outside Târgoviște, arrest about 5:50 p.m., the Târgoviște garrison (military unit UM 01417) about 6:10 p.m.; Sunday 24 December, 8 p.m.: the decision to create an Exceptional Military Tribunal; Monday 25 December, 1:20 p.m.: the trial opens; about 2:40 p.m.: death sentence "with right of appeal"; about 2:50 p.m.: the sentence is carried out. Backstory: Timișoara, Friday 15 - Wednesday 20 December 1989)

## How to read this file

- **[RECORD]**: primary/official: the trial transcript and sentence, the CFSN decision, Monitorul Oficial, Romanian diplomatic cables (CWIHP), US government texts (Bush Library, CIA), the 2006 presidential commission report (official report; its judgements are tagged CLAIM/REPORTED where they are opinion).
- **[REPORTED]**: historians (Petrescu), journalists' reconstructions (Haberman, Newsweek RO, EVZ, TVR), wire copy (AP, Reuters, AFP, RFE/RL).
- **[CLAIM]**: one person's or one side's assertion: the pilot's account, Ceaușescu's statements, prosecutors' allegations, Stănculescu/Voican/Iliescu self-accounts. Never narrate as fact.
- **[DISPUTED]**: sources disagree (each version given).
- **[COMPUTED]**: our arithmetic (time zones, sun, intervals, distances). Method shown.
- **[UNKNOWN]**: the record we read is silent; what would settle it is named.
- **(fetch)** = read through a page-summary tool. **(OCR)** = read in an archive.org OCR text. **(RO)** = Romanian, translated by us. **WIKI** = Wikipedia, lead-finder only.

Rules kept: no invented numbers, quotes or sources; UNKNOWN where the record is silent. Long quotes only from US government text (public domain) and Romanian official texts. Books and press: short quotes or paraphrase, attributed.

**Content flags for the edit (hard rules):**
- **The execution: one line of description, never images, never sound effects of gunfire on the moment, never the TV/film footage or stills from it.** Suggested line: "At about ten to three, in the barracks yard, the sentence was carried out." Nothing about the wall, the bodies, the number of shots.
- **No casualty images, ever** (the ~1,100 dead of December 1989). No hospital, morgue, cemetery-crosses-close-up of victims, no 1989 street photos with bodies or blood.
- **Elena Ceaușescu: narration only. NO image may show her (or any woman)** — this excludes most state-visit photos unless cropped (assets.md gives crop boxes). Her lines from the transcript may be **narrated**, not shown.
- **Romanian TV footage is NOT usable** (the 21 Dec live feed, the trial and execution film shot by Col. Ion Baiu, any TVR broadcast). Tell those moments with stills of the place + our own graphics.
- **"Terrorists":** always in quotation marks and attributed ("what the new authorities called 'terrorists'"). No one was ever tried as a "terrorist" (Tismăneanu Commission). Never say the Securitate did it, or that the army did it, as fact.
- **The Revolution case:** "prosecutors allege"; no verdict language; **Iliescu died on 5 Aug 2025 without ever being convicted**; Voican Voiculescu and Rus have not been convicted; the case was sent back to prosecutors twice (2021, Sept 2024).
- **No judgement words in narration:** not "tyrant", "butcher", "monster", "justice was done", "kangaroo court". Quote "tyrants" only as the prosecutor's word, "simulacrum of a trial" only as the 2006 commission's word.

## 5. What the record shows / what it doesn't

**Shows (RECORD):**
- The tribunal was created by a CFSN decision **dated 24 Dec 1989 and signed by Ion Iliescu** (CFSN241289).
- The session opened at **13:20** in **UM 01417 Târgoviște** (STENO header).
- The four charges and articles; the unanimous death sentence; **"with right of appeal"** written into the sentence; the communiqué saying it "became final and was carried out" (STENO; MO3).
- The announced charge of **"over 60,000 victims"** vs **1,104 dead in all** (162 before 22 Dec) per the 2006 presidential commission (TISM06 pp. 622-623).
- Ceaușescu refused to recognise the court and called it a coup (STENO).
- Moscow told Bucharest the crisis was its "own internal problem" an hour after the flight (CWIHP5).
- Washington condemned the Timișoara repression on 19 Dec and congratulated Iliescu on 27 Dec (FITZ).
- In the Revolution case, prosecutors' allegations, the indictments and the court rulings that sent the case back (REUTERS; RFERL240920).

**Doesn't show (or not in what we read):**
- **Who exactly decided the execution would follow immediately**: the 2006 commission says "the decision of immediate execution was taken in Bucharest by those who had taken power" (CLAIM: commission's finding, no document cited in the passage); TVR says Iliescu noted the decision and appointed Stănculescu (REPORTED). No document read orders the execution time.
- **Who the "terrorists" were**: no one was tried (TISM06); prosecutors allege a staged "psychosis" (CLAIM); the CIA (28 Dec) wrote of "Securitate diehards" (snippet, unread). **UNSETTLED.**
- **The exact minute** of take-off and of the execution (DISPUTED).
- **Whether the helicopter was really in danger** of being shot down (pilot's claim only).
- **What the couple were told on 22-24 Dec**, where they slept: UNKNOWN.
- **Siani-Davies's account** (unread) and **the indictment text** (unreachable).
