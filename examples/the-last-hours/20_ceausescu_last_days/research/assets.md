# Nicolae Ceaușescu's last days (Thursday 21 - Monday 25 December 1989, Bucharest / Târgoviște): visuals DOWNLOAD LIST (nothing downloaded)

Compiled 6 Nov 2026 for **The Last Hours**, folder 20_ceausescu_last_days. **Nothing has been downloaded to the project.** The owner approves every item.
- **Content checks** ("content check" column) were made by eye on 470 px contact sheets of the Commons thumbnails (built in memory; sheets kept in a temp folder outside the project, deleted after); Pexels clips on 3 of Pexels' own preview frames (frames 1, 7, 13). "Check" = could not be settled at 470 px: open at full size before use.
- **px** = pixel size from the Commons API (`imageinfo`); for PDFs, the page render size. **size** = byte size from the API, **re-verified by HTTP HEAD (Content-Length) on 6 Nov 2026: all 66 Commons URLs answered 200 and matched the API byte count exactly** ("HEAD ok"). Pexels sizes = HEAD Content-Length; durations read with ffprobe on the remote file (headers only). MB = 1,048,576 bytes.
- The **licence** cell names the Commons licence / template. Open the file page and confirm before downloading.
- **LESSONS 38 still applies:** before using any clip, make the contact sheet of the exact seconds used.

## HARD RULES applied
- **No women or girls in any visual.** Elena Ceaușescu is in most state-visit photos: those are either excluded or given a **crop box that removes her (and any other woman)**; verify every crop at full size. Crowd photos of 1989 where women cannot be ruled out are excluded or cropped to a part with no crowd.
- **No bodies, no injuries, no execution imagery, no casualty images.** None of the 60 items shows any. The execution wall at Târgoviște: **no free photo found on Commons** (and we would not show it as "the wall" anyway).
- **No Romanian TV footage or stills from it** (21 Dec live feed; trial/execution film by Col. Ion Baiu; TVR images on Google Arts & Culture are TVR collection items without a free licence: excluded).
- **No AI, no colourised versions** (the Commons "Nicolae Ceaușescu (color).png" is an altered colourisation: excluded).
- **No news-agency (AP/Getty/Reuters/AFP/Agerpres) or film/TV stills.**
- **Thumbnails: PD, CC0, CC BY, or attribution-only (FOCR/ComInRo) only; never CC BY-SA.**
