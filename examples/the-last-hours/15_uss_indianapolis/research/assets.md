Compiled 30 Oct 2026 for **The Last Hours**, folder 15_uss_indianapolis. **Nothing has been downloaded to the project.** The owner approves every item. Every still marked "me" was opened in the in-app browser and checked by eye (grid views at 170-400 px per image; the key people shots at 800 px). Films were checked with seek-point contact sheets (16 frames each, 240 px; the sheets were temporary and have been deleted).
- **px** is the pixel size of that exact URL, read from the file header (a ≤384 KB read held in memory, never saved), the Commons API or ffprobe on the remote file. For a few NHHC TIFFs the header sits at the end of the file: those say "TIFF, px not read".
- **size** is the byte count of that exact URL (HTTP Content-Length / Content-Range, Commons API or ffprobe). MB = 1,048,576 bytes.
- The **licence** cell links to the source page. Check it before downloading.
- **check** = one-line content check (women / bodies / wounds) and who checked it.
- **ALREADY ON DISK** = in an earlier episode folder; 0 MB. Copy it, don't re-download.

## Licence key
- **PD, US federal government work:**
  - **NARA still pictures** in RG 80 (80-G, 80-G-K), RG 19 (19-LCM), RG 77 (77-BT): NARA use status **"Unrestricted"** (checked through the catalog API on 80-G-490321, 80-G-490324, 80-G-346866, 80-G-490312, 19-LCM-CA35-86911, 77-BT-114).
  - **NHHC photos and documents** ("Official U.S. Navy Photograph"; NHHC pages say "This photo has been released and is available for public use"). The NHHC transcription PDFs are scans of Navy records in NARA RG 38 and NHHC archives.
  - **NARA RG 428 Navy films (428-NPC)**: Navy-produced; NARA marks use status **"Undetermined"** (a catalog flag, not a claim). Low risk; keep the NARA ID in the description.
  - ***A Grave Misfortune*** (NHHC/GPO 2018): a US government publication. Its reproduced photos are the same Navy/NARA images; one is **not** PD: the wreck anchor photo "courtesy of Paul G. Allen" (X1).
- **Commons:** used only where the file page says PD and names a US government source. Exceptions are flagged.
- **Japanese material:** Hashimoto's attack sketch (A9) is a Japanese naval document published by NHHC with translation; low risk as a document image; credit "Mochitsura Hashimoto, NIDS Tokyo, via NHHC".
- **Pexels** (ALREADY ON DISK only): free use; label "illustrative".
