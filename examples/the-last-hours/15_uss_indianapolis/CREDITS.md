# File 15: USS Indianapolis (30 July - 3 August 1945): credits

Real images only. Nothing shown of the dead, of wounds, burns or bites, and no sharks; no women on screen (the Navy films and photos with
nurses, the funeral photo and the wound portraits are excluded: research/assets.md section X). The Navy rescue film is cut to the
seconds that show only the sea, rafts, a plane and men. Only the items below appear in the edit (render_indy.py SHOTS).

## U.S. Navy / Naval History and Heritage Command (public domain, US government works)
- Documents (NHHC "Documents Relating to Loss of USS Indianapolis (CA-35)", scans of NARA RG 38 / RG 24 and NHHC Archives):
  court of inquiry findings, Guam, August 1945; Captain McVay's action report, 26 August 1945; routing instructions PDG 1849 and the
  intelligence brief, Port Director Guam, 27-28 July 1945; ULTRA extracts (COMNAVSECGRU file 5830/114); casualty summation, August 1945;
  COMINCH chart-room Pacific dispatches, 2-3 August 1945; deck log of USS LST-779, 29-30 July 1945; Secretary of the Navy Gordon R. England,
  11 July 2001; Route "Peddie" chart (court of inquiry exhibit); a Navy chart of her track from Guam.
- Commander Mochitsura Hashimoto's own sketch of the attack, 29 July 1945 (National Institute for Defense Studies, Tokyo, via NHHC).
- Photographs: USS Indianapolis at sea, 1943-44 (NH 124466); her crew in the well deck (80-G-336932); I-58 off Sasebo, April 1946 (80-G-260244);
  Building 57, Washington Navy Yard (NH 95295); USS Ringness (NH 73857).
- USS Cecil J. Doyle (DE-368), war diary, August 1945, and a photo of the ship, c. 1945 (via Wikimedia Commons).
