Compiled 6 Nov 2026 for **The Last Hours**, folder 19_glenn_miller. **Nothing has been downloaded to the project.** The owner approves every item.
- **Content checks** ("check" column) were made by eye on preview images (Commons 400-px thumbnails, Pexels 400-px previews, NARA scans viewed at 700-1,000 px with crops of faces/figures); films on seek frames pulled from the archive.org stream (every 10-15 s over the range given). Previews were held in a temporary folder outside the project, `D:\_claude_tmp_19_glenn_miller` (19 MB: previews, crops, scripts); the automatic delete was blocked by a safety check, so **delete that folder by hand**. "Unchecked" = not looked at.
- **px** = pixel size from the Commons API imageinfo (Commons items), or read from the file header (NARA, archive.org, Pexels at the `w=1920` size we list). **size** = HTTP HEAD Content-Length, checked 6 Nov 2026 (every URL below returned 200). MB = 1,048,576 bytes.
- The **licence** cell names the Commons template / source terms. Open the file page and confirm before downloading.
- Commons file page = `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:` + the file name at the end of the direct URL (given in full for each item).
- **LESSONS 38 still applies:** before using any clip, make the contact sheet of the exact seconds used.
- **HARD RULES applied:** no women/girls in any image (checked; anything with a woman or an unclear small figure is in section X); no bodies or injuries; no AI images; no film/TV stills (*The Glenn Miller Story*, 1954, is out); no news-agency photos; **no Glenn Miller recordings or band audio of any kind** (mute every film; archive.org's Miller records marked "public domain" are NOT safe: Content ID).

## Best 15: in order of use
1. **A2 MACR 10770, report form (hi-res microfilm scan)**: cold open. Spotlight + underline, one at a time: "WEATHER... Unknown", "Time 1355", "Location Twinwood", "Other Circumstances: Unknown". (A1 is the cleaner gif of the same page.)

4. **A12 Twinwood Farm from the air, 28 Mar 1948** (RAF): push in to the runway intersection; we add "Runway 23" and "1:55 p.m.". Label "1948".
5. **E12 fog over English fields** + **E16 frost and fog on a field edge**: "early morning at Twinwood: 250-foot ceiling, freezing drizzle".
6. **C13 Twinwood control tower** (restored): "the tower that watched them leave". Label "today".
7. **C3 UC-64 43-5116 (wartime olive drab, B&W)** + **C2 YC-64A**: the takeoff beat (no film of 44-70285 exists; TIGHAR says no photo of it is known).
8. **E2 fog over a flat grey sea (B&W)** → **E1**: the crossing.
9. **E3 Beachy Head cliffs and lighthouse, overcast**: "2:33 p.m.: a single-engine plane reported over Beachy Head".

11. **A8 Eisenhower's cable, 22 Dec 1944**: spotlight "1800A hours 24 December" and "No trace of this airplane can be found".
12. **A9 Casualty telegram, 23 Dec 1944** (crop to the message lines): "MISSING IN FLIGHT SINCE FIFTEEN DECEMBER BETWEEN ENGLAND AND PARIS".
13. **V1 Lancasters over the sea (Universal Newsreel, 22 Nov 1944), 0:03-0:20** + **C17 a 4,000-lb "cookie" falling** (IWM): theory 1. Label "Lancasters, November 1944".
14. **A13 Pratt & Whitney R-1340 engine** + **E10 frost on glass**: theory 2 (icing). Label "the same engine type".
15. **A17 the Tablets of the Missing, Cambridge American Cemetery** (CC BY-SA, in-video only) or **A16 memorial headstone, Arlington**: end.

## Licence key
- **PD-USGov** (NARA, US Army, USAF, War Department): public domain. NARA lists the MACR and the 342-FH photos as "Use: Unrestricted". **The OMPF (A8-A10) is marked "Use: Restricted - Possibly"** (NARA's general privacy flag on personnel files); the pages used are 1944 War Department messages. Confirm on the catalog page.
- **M1380 microfilm scan (A2): "Image provided by Ancestry"**: a NARA partner scan published on the NARA catalog as Unrestricted. If in doubt, use A1 (NARA's own gif) instead.
- **PD-UKGov:** UK Crown copyright expired. **IWM wartime photos (C17-C22):** Crown copyright, taken 1940s, expired in the UK before 1996 → also PD in the US. **A12 (RAF aerial, 1948):** PD in the UK since 1999; because it was still in copyright in the UK on 1 Jan 1996, **its US status is uncertain** (URAA). Fine as a short map backdrop; do not make it the thumbnail.
- **PD-US-no notice (ad) / PD-US-no-notice:** published in the US without a copyright notice (B5: Billboard 1943 Music Yearbook ad; B6: provenance weak, "pub?").

- **CC0:** credit as courtesy. **CC BY 2.0** (C7): credit line required. **CC BY-SA 2.0/3.0/4.0:** credit + licence name on screen; inside the video only, **never in the thumbnail**.
