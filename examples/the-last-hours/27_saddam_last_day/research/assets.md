## HARD RULES applied
- **No women or girls in any visual.** Excluded: the 28 Dec 2006 Crawford walk (Condoleezza Rice in frame), the 2004 "Crowds outside Camp Justice" (pilgrim crowd), the 1916 Kadhimiya gate (veiled figures), Pexels clips with people. Crowd/formation photos where a woman cannot be ruled out at 470 px carry a **crop box** or "check".
- **No execution imagery of any kind:** no gallows, noose, execution room, Iraqiya video or stills, leaked phone video or stills, masked hangmen, body, shroud, coffin-at-the-PM's-office TV frames, grave photos with mourners. **None of the items below shows any of these.**
- **No degrading capture images:** the "spider hole" photo (Saddam pinned to the ground) and the dental/medical-exam frames are excluded. The two capture portraits kept (B5, B6) are DoD identity photos, face only.
- **Real photos and documents only:** no AI, no colourised or "edited" versions for thumbnails (B2 "edit1" is a cleaned crop, flagged).
- **No news-agency (AP/Reuters/Getty/AFP) or pool photos of the Dujail trial (2005-06)** — none found under a free licence. The only free court photos are the **1 July 2004** first hearing (DoD, PD): always label "July 2004".
- **Thumbnails: PD, CC0, CC BY only; never CC BY-SA**, never PD-Iraq / Iraqi News Agency items (licence doubtful in the US).
