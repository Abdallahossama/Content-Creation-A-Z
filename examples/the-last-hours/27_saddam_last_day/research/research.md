# SADDAM HUSSEIN'S LAST DAY: verified research (Baghdad, Friday 29 - Sunday 31 December 2006. Friday ~11:55 p.m.: Prime Minister Maliki signs the order "to carry out the hanging until death"; Saturday 30 December 1:30 a.m.: fourteen officials are called; ~3:30 a.m.: they gather / are driven to the helicopter pad; about 5:00 a.m.: US military police take Saddam from Camp Cropper by helicopter; 5:30 a.m.: Iraq takes full custody (NYT); about 6:00 a.m.: the verdict is read, he is led to the gallows room; about 6:05-6:10 a.m.: the sentence is carried out (6:10 per the NYT); sunrise 7:05 a.m., the first day of Eid al-Adha for Iraq's Sunnis; Saturday night - Sunday 31 December: a US Black Hawk carries the coffin north; burial at Awja about 3:30-4:00 a.m. Legal path: Dujail, Thursday 8 July 1982 → Revolutionary Court, 14 June 1984 → Saddam's ratification decree no. 778, 16 June 1984 → Iraqi High Tribunal verdict, Sunday 5 November 2006 → Appeals Chamber, Tuesday 26 December 2006)

Channel: The Last Hours (@TheLastHoursDocs; US / Canada / UK audience, 35+, mostly TV). Format: hour-by-hour countdown with the clock times the record has, told only from the record. Folder 27_saddam_last_day. Narrator: the File Keeper. Length about 10-12 min (85-100 spoken lines). Release **Sun 27 Dec 2026**, three days before the **20th anniversary (Wed 30 Dec 2026)** [COMPUTED: CAL]. Ecosystem: sits next to our #1 file "Saddam Hussein Called a Meeting. 66 Names Were Read Out." (1979 purge; 87% of its views from Suggested beside big Iraq/Saddam videos).

Spine: **Thu 8 Jul 1982** shots at Saddam's motorcade in Dujail → ~800 detained → **Sun 27 May 1984** Saddam refers 148 men and boys to the Revolutionary Court → **Thu 14 Jun 1984** all 148 sentenced to death → **Sat 16 Jun 1984** Saddam signs Presidential Decree no. 778 ratifying the sentences → most executed **March 1985** → **Sat 13 Dec 2003** captured near Tikrit → **Thu 1 Jul 2004** first court appearance → **Wed 19 Oct 2005** Dujail trial opens → **Thu 27 Jul 2006** trial closes → **Sun 5 Nov 2006** verdict: death by hanging (letter dated that day) → **Tue 26 Dec** appeal rejected; "It cannot exceed 30 days" → **Wed 27 Dec** farewell letter posted → **Thu 28 Dec** US and Iraqi officials meet; half-brothers visit his cell (reported) → **Fri 29 Dec** day of "legal workarounds"; evening: US judge in Washington refuses a stay; late-night dinner at the PM's office; **~11:55 p.m.** Maliki signs → **Sat 30 Dec 1:30 a.m.** witnesses called → **3:30 a.m.** gathered → **~5:00** Saddam leaves Camp Cropper in US custody → **5:30** Iraqi custody → **~6:00** verdict read, walk to the gallows room → **6:05-6:10** hanged → **7:05** sunrise; Eid begins → **~10 a.m.** (reported) state TV → **Sat evening** coffin collected at the PM's office; leaked phone video online "late Saturday" → **Sun 31 Dec ~3:30-4:00 a.m.** burial at Awja → **Wed 3 Jan 2007** guard arrested; US: "we would have done it differently" → **Mon 15 Jan 2007** co-defendants hanged; US cable on the execution → **Tue 16 Jan 2007** Bush: "kind of a revenge killing".

Compiled 14 Nov 2026. Today is **7,259 days** after the execution; release day (Sun 27 Dec 2026) is **7,302 days** after it (19 years, 11 months, 27 days) [COMPUTED: CAL].

## How to read this file

- **[RECORD]**: primary/official: US government statements, the State Department cable, court records (US District Court order; IHT judgment numbers and dates as reported), the CRS report, HRW's reading of the trial documents (marked HRW).
- **[REPORTED]**: journalists' reconstructions (NYT, WaPo, AP, Reuters, BBC, VOA, CBC, AFP).
- **[CLAIM]**: one person's or one side's assertion (a witness, an Iraqi official, Saddam, his lawyers). Never narrate as fact.
- **[DISPUTED]**: sources disagree (each version given).
- **[COMPUTED]**: our arithmetic (time zones, sun, intervals, distances). Method shown.
- **[UNKNOWN]**: the record we read is silent; what would settle it is named.
- **(fetch)** = read through a page-summary tool. **(raw)** = full text read (curl + text extraction). **WIKI** = Wikipedia, lead-finder only.

Rules kept: no invented numbers, quotes or sources; UNKNOWN where the record is silent. Long quotes only from US government text (public domain). Press and HRW: short quotes or paraphrase, attributed.

**Content flags for the edit (hard rules):**
- **The hanging: one line, never shown, never heard.** Suggested line: "At about ten past six, the trapdoor opened." Nothing about the drop, the rope, the neck, the sound, how long he hung, the body.
- **No images of the gallows, the noose, the execution room, the official or leaked video, or any frame from either. No images of the body or the shroud** (Iraqi TV released them; we never use them). Describe the body only as "the coffin" / "his body was flown north".
- **Never play or quote the audio of the leaked video beyond the words already in print** ("Muqtada", "Go to hell", "Is this manhood?"); narrate them as quotes from the witnesses and newspapers.
- **No women on screen.** Women in this story (Ambassador Margaret Scobey; Maliki adviser Mariam al-Rayes; Judge Colleen Kollar-Kotelly; Saddam's daughter Raghad; observer Miranda Sissons; Bint al-Huda) are **narration only**.
- **No celebration, no sectarian framing as fact.** The guards' chants are the witnesses' account; "Shiite guards" only as the newspapers' description, attributed.
- **No judgement words** in narration: not "tyrant", "monster", "butcher", "justice was done", "lynching", "murder". Quote "fair trial" only as Bush's words, "fundamentally unfair" only as HRW's, "hastily run" only as the US cable's, "revenge killing" only as Bush's.

## 5. What the record shows / what it doesn't

**Shows (RECORD):**
- The US held **physical** custody to the end at Iraq's request; Iraq held **legal** custody (DoD 29 Dec; Caldwell 3 Jan).
- The US flew the 14 witnesses, searched them and took their phones; transported Saddam; transferred custody inside the holding-cell building; had nobody in the room or courtyard; helped move the remains (CABLE146; AFPS070103).
- Americans had built the execution platform at the site (cable: "EmbOff confirmed ... Americans constructed the execution platform") — **sensitive; see 12**.
- The timing argument: "if it finished before sunrise it would not coincide with Eid" (al-Faroon, CABLE146).
- Bush's statement of 29 Dec ("a fair trial"), his 4 Jan ("more dignified way") and 16 Jan ("kind of a revenge killing") words.
- The 1984 chain: referral 27 May, sentence 14 June, Decree 778 of 16 June (HRW reading trial documents).

**Doesn't show (or not in what we read):**
- **Who filmed the leaked video.** Al-Faroon saw "two government officials" filming; a Justice Ministry guard was arrested; the SUV with six "video personnel" was not searched inside. **UNSETTLED.**
- **The exact minute** of death (6:00-6:10).
- **Where exactly the US handed him over** (cable vs WaPo).
- **Whether the US pushed for a delay**: NYT (Iraqi and US officials) says the Americans "counseled caution"; an unnamed Iraqi official says they wanted 15 days; a senior US official told NYT on 30 Dec "there was no desire from the United States to seek a delay". **DISPUTED.**
- **Where the body is now** (after 2014-15): UNKNOWN.
- **The IHT judgments' own words** (not read).

## 11. Do-not-say list

- "The Americans executed Saddam" / "the CIA hanged him" → "Iraqi officials carried out the sentence; US forces held and transported him".
- "Executed on Christmas / on Eid" (as fact) → "an hour before the holiday began for Iraq's Sunnis".
- "He was hanged for gassing the Kurds" → "for Dujail".
- "148 people were massacred" (flat) → "148 men and boys were sentenced; dozens may already have died in custody".
- "Shiite death squads hanged him", "the Mahdi Army ran the execution" → attributed descriptions only; al-Faroon said "official prison guards".
- Any description of the drop, the sound, the neck, the body, how long it hung, the shroud, blood.
- "His last word was Muhammad" (as fact, from the leaked video) → "he did not finish the prayer" (WaPo/AP), or omit.
- "Fair trial" / "sham trial" in our voice.
- "Saddam's grave is at Awja" (present tense).
- "Bush watched it" → he was asleep (NYT).

## 12. YouTube safety notes

- **Violent content:** real execution; one line, no visuals, no audio, no SFX on the moment (silence or a clock tick). No thumbnails with ropes, gallows or the masked hangmen.
- **Third-party footage / Content ID:** **No Iraqiya footage, no leaked phone video, no stills from either, no AP/Reuters/Getty pool photos of the trial.**
- **Sectarian sensitivity:** chants are quoted as witnesses heard them; avoid "Shiite thugs", "Sunni martyr"; Maliki, Sadr and Rubaie are public figures: attribute everything.
- **The US platform detail** (cable: Americans built the platform): true to the record but easy to misread as "the US built the gallows for Saddam". If used: "The cable notes that Americans had built the platform at the site, earlier, for Iraq's executions" — or leave it out (decision 4).
- **Leaked cable:** quoting a WikiLeaks-published classified cable is common in news; quote short lines, attribute "a US diplomatic cable later published by WikiLeaks".
- **Copycat deaths** (children, Jan 2007): **do not mention** (self-harm content risk).
