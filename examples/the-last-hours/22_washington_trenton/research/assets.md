## Best 15: opening minute, then the night (in order of use)
1. **E8 candle in the dark** + **A7 a Washington letter in his own hand (April 1776)**: cold open, "6 p.m., at the ferry, he wrote: the night is favourable." Label A7 "Washington's handwriting, 1776" (it is not the Christmas note).
2. **E1 / E2 pancake ice on a river** + **C5 Leutze's painted ice (detail)**: "by Wednesday the river was full of ice".
3. **B1 Charles Willson Peale, *George Washington*, 1776** (CC0 photo): the face as he looked that year; title card.
4. **A2 Faden's 1777 "Plan of the operations of General Washington"**: push in from Bristol to McConkey's Ferry to Trenton (we add pins).
5. **A5 *The American Crisis*, 1776 printing, first page**: "These are the times that try men's souls."
6. **B12 Thomas Paine** (Sharp after Romney, 1793).
7. **A6 Cadwalader's own letter, Bristol, 24 Dec 1776** (a real Christmas Eve letter from the planners; label "Christmas Eve 1776").
8. **C23 Durham boats at Washington Crossing** (CC BY-SA, in-video only): "boats like these".
9. **C22 McConkey's Ferry Inn** (CC BY-SA, in-video only): "the ferry house is still there".
10. **E5 heavy snowfall at night**: ~11 p.m., the nor'easter.
11. **E6 bonfire**: Greenwood's fence-rail fire, "my back would be freezing".
12. **C1 Leutze, *Washington Crossing the Delaware* (1851)**, crops C2/C4: "the painting everyone knows; it was painted 75 years later".
13. **A1 Lt. Wiederholdt's 1776 sketch of the engagement** (LoC): 8:00 a.m., the outpost.
14. **C11 Trumbull, *The Capture of the Hessians at Trenton*** (Yale): ~9 a.m.
15. **B3 Trumbull, *General George Washington at Trenton*** (Yale, 1792): the ending / deadline chapter.
