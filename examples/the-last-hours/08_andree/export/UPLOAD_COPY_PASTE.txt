-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Andrée balloon expedition, 1897: three Swedes lifted off from an Arctic island in a hydrogen balloon to fly over the North Pole. Within minutes the ropes they meant to steer with were lying on the beach. Thirty-three years later, two sealers looking for drinking water found their camp, their diaries, and a roll of film nobody had developed.

This file follows them hour by hour from the record: the vote on the balloon-house roof, "Cut everywhere!", 36 carrier pigeons and the one that was shot off a ship's mast, the 65 hours in the air, the march across ice that drifted the other way, White Island, Strindberg's last note ("home, 7:05 a.m."), the 1930 find, the film developed 33 years late, and every theory of how they died. None has been proven.
