# The Andrée balloon expedition (1897; found 1930): visuals DOWNLOAD LIST (nothing downloaded)

Compiled 23 Oct 2026 for **The Last Hours**, folder 08_andree. **Nothing has been downloaded into the project.** The owner approves every item. (Preview copies were opened in a temp folder outside the project only, to check each picture by eye, zooming into every group of people.)
- **px** is the pixel size of that exact URL: Internet Archive / Tekniska JPEGs and newspaper JP2s read from the file header; Commons from the Commons API; Runeberg page scans from the file; NASA snapshots = the size requested in the URL.
- **size** comes from an HTTP HEAD (Content-Length) on that exact URL (Internet Archive JPEGs, newspaper JP2s). Wikimedia Commons answered 429 to bursts, so Commons sizes are the API byte count (identical to HEAD wherever HEAD succeeded). Project Runeberg sends no Content-Length, and the two Internet Archive JP2s inside a zip (A3, A4) are served through a redirect: byte count of a GET. NASA Worldview snapshots are generated: byte count of a GET. MB = 1,048,576 bytes.
- The **licence** cell links to the record to check before downloading: the DigitaltMuseum page for Tekniska museet items (blocked to our tools; the licence was read from the Arkivkopia mirror, which copies DigitaltMuseum's own licence field), the Commons file page, the Runeberg page, the Internet Archive item.
- **MOMP** = *Med Örnen mot polen* (1930), page numbers as printed (Runeberg file number = printed page − 1).

## Best 5 for the thumbnail (one strong real image filling the frame + 2 short text lines)
1. **C1, the Eagle on the ice** (Strindberg, 14 July 1897; retouched 1930 print, 3543×2374): a huge black sphere, two tiny men, white snow. Reads instantly on a phone. Text on the empty sky: "DEVELOPED / 33 YEARS LATER". Pairs with title 1.
2. **C10, all three men shoving the boat-sledge over a pressure ridge** (self-timer): faces, effort, flying snow; the only frame with all three in action. Text: "1897 / FOUND 1930".
3. **B13, the balloon just after take-off**, skimming the harbour with a long white wake: the moment it went wrong (the steering ropes were already on the beach).
4. **A3, Andrée's pigeon** (1897 plate, the shot bird mounted, wings spread): one object, no people. Text: "ALL WELL / ON BOARD" (the message's own words). Pairs with title 2. Remove the fold crease.
5. **F2, White Island in the drift ice** (NASA/USGS Landsat, 9 Aug 2021): a white island in a black sea of floes. Strong mood; text "THEY REACHED / THIS ISLAND".

Alternatives: C9 (Andrée alone on the gondola scanning for land), C2 (the raw, streaked frame: eerie and honest), C17 (the ghost frame).

**Portrait verdict:** public-domain studio portraits exist for all three men (Florman, 1896-97): B1 (the three together, 3 May 1897), B2 (Andrée), B3 (Strindberg); Frænkel can be cropped from B1 or shown in B4. **No image of Anna Charlier or any woman is listed.**

## Licence key
- **Public domain everywhere:** everything photographed or written by Andrée, Strindberg or Frænkel (all d. 1897); 1896-97 photos by Florman (d. 1900), Hasselblad, Knös, Svedenborg (d. 1943), Hamberg (d. 1933); the 1897 *Ymer* plates; 1897 US newspapers. Swedish photographs taken before 1969 that are not artistic "works" are free in Sweden; works published before 1931 are public domain in the US (works of 1930 entered the US public domain on 1 Jan 2026).
- **Public Domain Mark 1.0** is how Tekniska museet (and Grenna Museum for H19) label these scans on DigitaltMuseum.
- **NASA** (Worldview, MODIS, HLS/Landsat): public domain; don't use agency logos.
- **CC0 / No known restrictions:** Richard Neill's Kvitøya photo (CC0), National Library of Norway and National Library of Ireland Flickr Commons items, Nationaal Archief.
- **In H only:** 1930 photos by Norwegian expedition members (Sørensen, unknown): PD in the US, status in Norway/UK depends on unknown death dates; CC BY / CC BY-SA (credit lines in the rows); ESA Sentinel (attribution); 1930 maps by Generalstabens Litografiska Anstalt; crowds; drawings.
- **Grenna Museum – Andréexpeditionen Polarcenter** (holds most finds): website refused connection on 23 Oct 2026; its DigitaltMuseum items are labelled individually (some "Copyright" or "Contact owner"). Only one Grenna item is listed (H19, PD Mark). For anything else from Gränna, ask the museum.
- **Moderna Museet** holds 1930 prints marked "© Nils Strindberg" on its website: not used; the same frames are listed from Tekniska museet (PD Mark).

## Hard rules applied (everything in A-F passed them)
- **No women or girls.** Every image was opened and checked, zooming into groups. People in A-F: the three expedition men; ground crew and sailors at Danes Island (B10, B12); Svedenborg (B11); six named men of the 1930 commission (D3); men at the 1931 exhibition (D6, crop the blurred figure at the left edge); sailors on *Svensksund* (D4); unreadable specks in boats (D5, E4: crop). Excluded or flagged: Anna Charlier portraits; the 1896 and 1897 Gothenburg departure crowds; the 1930 Gothenburg and Stockholm crowds (H19, procession excluded); a 1930s photo of schoolgirls at the exhibition; a group photo with Nordenskiöld and women guests (Tekniska TEKA0092997); the Storkyrkan congregation (H20, crop).
- **No remains, nothing gory.** No photo of a body, bone or grave contents is listed. Excluded: MOMP p. 252 (Andrée's body), p. 324 and p. 258 (*Isbjørn* camp photos: a white object that may be bone), p. 316 (camp after the melt: not risked). H9 (the grave: stones only) and H7/H10 (objects found with Andrée) are flagged as sensitive. The newspaper in A12: headline only.
- **Animals:** C15 (two men and a shot polar bear) is the only one in the main list; C17 shows the same scene as an unreadable ghost image. I2 and I3 are alternates. Use briefly.
- **Real only.** Period photographs, real documents and maps, NASA/USGS satellite images. Photochroms (B6, E4) are 1890s colour prints made from real photographs: label them. The one drawing in the Tekniska set is in H22. Excluded: *Gartenlaube* engravings, the "railway station" illustration, caricatures, a carnival ticket, a board game, Troell film stills, AI or colourised versions.
- **Truth.** Retouched vs raw frames are labelled; mirror-reversed scans flagged (H23); Tekniska's "Vitön" place tag on ice-march frames is wrong (they were on the drifting ice).

Download one at a time.
- **Wikimedia Commons**: answered 429 to bursts; download one at a time, a few seconds apart.
- **Project Runeberg** (*Med Örnen mot polen*): `/img/medornen/NNNN.1.jpg` is the full-resolution page scan (about 2,300×3,600 px, 300 dpi). Crop the photo out of the page; captions are printed under each photo.
- **Internet Archive JP2 inside a zip** (A3, A4): the link redirects to a `view_archive.php` URL that serves the single JP2; convert with ffmpeg or any JP2 reader.
- **Newspapers** (A10-A12): South Carolina Digital Newspaper Program mirror of Chronicling America (loc.gov returned 403). The `.jp2` is the full page; a `.pdf` of the page exists at the same path with `.pdf` (0.7-1.9 MB).
- **NASA Worldview**: snapshot URLs are generated images (same URL = same picture). EPSG:3413 frames: north points left.
- **grenna.se**: connection refused (23 Oct 2026).
