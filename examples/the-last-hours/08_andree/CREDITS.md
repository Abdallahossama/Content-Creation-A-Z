# The Last Hours, file 07: the Andrée balloon expedition (1897; found 1930): credits

## Narration
The creator's own voice, cloned locally (Chatterbox Turbo, MIT). Script: `vo_lines.txt`; every fact from `research/research.md`.
Quotes from the diaries, almanac, letters and messages are **our own translations** from *Med Örnen mot polen* (Stockholm: Bonniers, 1930;
the writers died in 1897, so their words are public domain). The 1931 English translation (*The Andrée Diaries*) was not quoted.
