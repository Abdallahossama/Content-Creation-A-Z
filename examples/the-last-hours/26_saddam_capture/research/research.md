## How to read this file

- **[RECORD]**: primary/official: DoD transcripts, CENTCOM/CJTF-7 orders and reports (FOIA), White House texts, FBI interview summaries, DoD captions.
- **[REPORTED]**: journalists' reconstructions, official news-service stories written after the fact (AFPS/DVIDS, army.mil), soldiers' accounts relayed to reporters.
- **[CLAIM]**: one person's or one side's assertion: Saddam's words as relayed, the farmer's film account, staging theories, the interpreter's later claims. Never narrate as fact.
- **[DISPUTED]**: sources disagree (each version given).
- **[COMPUTED]**: our arithmetic (time zones, sun/moon, intervals, distances). Method shown.
- **[UNKNOWN]**: the record we read is silent; what would settle it is named.
- Times: **EXACT** = a clock time printed in a primary source (source named). **APPROX** = "about/approximately" in the source. **COMPUTED** = ours.

Rules kept: no invented numbers, quotes or sources; UNKNOWN where the record is silent. US government texts are public domain (long quotes allowed); press: short quotes or paraphrase, attributed.

**Content flags for the edit (hard rules):**
- **No bodies, ever.** Uday and Qusay (22 Jul 2003): one line of narration, **no photos** (the DoD released body photos; never use them or anything near them). The execution (30 Dec 2006): **one line, no footage, no stills, no rope, no sound**. Suggested line: "Three years later, on December 30th, 2006, he was executed in Baghdad."
- **Blood:** the Samir photo (Commons "SaddamSpiderHole.jpg") shows Saddam pinned to the ground with blood on his face: **excluded** (also doubtful licence). The DoD mug shot 031214-D-0000X-001 shows a small cut above the left eyebrow: usable, but no zoom on the cut.
- **No women** in any visual (his wife Sajida and daughters: narration only, and only if needed).
- **The medical-exam video**: show it **only as it appears on the screen behind Bremer** (DoD photo 031214-F-1093P-003). We have no free copy of the clip itself, and it was criticised as humiliating (Vatican official: "like a cow", NYT 16 Dec 2003). One line, no mocking SFX.
- **"Caught like a rat"** is Odierno's phrase: quote it once, attributed, never as our voice.
- **No judgement words** in narration ("coward", "rat", "butcher"). Let the record speak: "a pistol he did not use" (Odierno; Rumsfeld).
- **Living people:** Eric Maddox, George Piro, Samir, Alaa Namiq, Steve Russell, James Hickey: attribute, no thumbnails.

## 5. What the record shows / what it doesn't

**Shows (RECORD):**
- The clock: ~10:50 tip, ~18:00 move, ~20:00 assault, ~21:15 secure area (Sanchez, DOD1214); 8:26 p.m. (Odierno); 2030C (CJTF-7 FRAGOs, 14 and 18 Dec).
- Objective names Wolverine I and II; ~600 soldiers; no shots; two AK-47s, a pistol, $750,000 in $100s, a taxi; two other men (DOD1214).
- The announcement was ordered for **1500C** by FRAGO 1217, issued **1410C** (CC-FOIA).
- The first interrogation report is stamped **2120Z** (TIR001); his water answer.
- Bush was told at ~3:15 p.m. EST Sat (Rumsfeld) and 5:14 a.m. EST Sun (Rice) (WH1214A).
- Within days, CENTCOM itself recorded rumours: "body double" (Al Arabiya) and "some Iraqis still do not believe" (CAG1216; FRAGO1239).
- In FBI custody he called the informant a "traitor" and said the farm was his 1959 hiding place (FBI-S1, FBI-S2).

**Doesn't show (or not in what we read):**
- **The exact words at the hole** in any primary document. They are second-hand (officers relaying soldiers), first published 15-16 Dec 2003.
- **How long he was in the hole** that night, or how long he had lived at that farm. Odierno: "probably there a short period of time, but I couldn't tell you how long" (his own "nothing official").
- **Who first saw the lid** (three versions).
- **Al-Musslit's later fate** (2026: UNKNOWN).
- **The names of the two men detained** (only "Qais Namiq Jassem and his brothers" as farm owners in TIR001).
- **Whether the $25 million was ever paid** to anyone (reports: not paid).
