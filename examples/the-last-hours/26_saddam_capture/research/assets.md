## HARD RULES applied
- **No women or girls.** Every item below that shows people shows men only (checked by eye). No crowd photos.
- **No bodies, no gore, no execution imagery.** No Uday/Qusay photos (the DoD body photos are excluded by rule and not listed). The famous "Samir pinning Saddam" photo (blood on his face) is **excluded** (X section). Saddam's DoD mug shot B3 shows a small cut above the eyebrow: allowed, no zoom on it.
- **Real photos and documents only.** No AI, no cartoons, no the 2003 "spider hole" newspaper diagram (it is a British press graphic; copyright and "meme" baggage): **excluded**.
- **No network TV** (CNN/Fox/NBC/Al Jazeera), no documentary stills (*Hiding Saddam Hussein*, History Channel), no agency photos (AP/Reuters/Getty/AFP).
- **Thumbnails: PD / CC0 / CC BY only; never CC BY-SA.**
