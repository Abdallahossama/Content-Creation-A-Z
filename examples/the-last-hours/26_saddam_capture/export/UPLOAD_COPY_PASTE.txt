DESCRIPTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ad-Dawr, near Tikrit, Iraq, Saturday, December 13, 2003: at about 10:50 in the morning the U.S. Army got the tip. At 6 p.m. about 600 soldiers moved out in the dark. At 8:26 p.m., under a foam lid in a walled yard, two hands came up out of a hole. Saddam Hussein had been on the run for 248 days. This is the file, hour by hour, from the record.

You'll see who gave him up, what was in the hut and in the hole ($750,000 in $100 bills, two rifles, a pistol he never fired), the first interrogation report written that night (Question 4: "Would you like some water?"), the "We got him" press conference, the rumours that followed, the $25 million reward, and what Saddam later told the FBI about the same farm in 1959. No images of the sons' bodies, the medical exam or the execution are shown.

An educational documentary built from declassified U.S. records (CENTCOM FOIA case 05-0109: the J2X tactical interrogation report, FRAGO 1217 and 1239, the CAG information paper), the FBI interview reports published by the National Security Archive, Department of Defense briefings and photographs, and contemporary reporting.
