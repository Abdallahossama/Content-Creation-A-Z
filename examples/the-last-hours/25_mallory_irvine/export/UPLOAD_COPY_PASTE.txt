Sunday, June 8, 1924, 12:50 p.m. Noel Odell looks up through a break in the clouds and sees George Mallory and Andrew Irvine high on the northeast ridge of Mount Everest, "going strong for the top." Then the clouds close in, and nobody ever sees them alive again. Seventy-five years later, a search team finds Mallory on the north face. What was in his pockets, and what was missing, still divides climbers today. Did they reach the summit 29 years before Hillary and Tenzing?
