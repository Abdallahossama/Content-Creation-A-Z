# Ep 9 "What Walking Every Day Does To Your Organs" — research (agent, 18 Oct 2026)

Not medical advice: we explain studies. Corrections: "60% more creative" is from Stanford's press release (paper: 81% improved on one test, 23% on another). Buffey 2022 = walking breaks during long sitting, NOT after-meal walks (use Reynolds 2016, DiPietro 2013 for after-meal).

Myths: (1) "You need 10,000 steps" (rows 23-26). (2) "Short walks don't count" — 2018 US guidelines committee dropped the 10-minute-bout rule (Jakicic et al., MSSE 2019). (3) "Walking wears out your knees" (row 21).

Hooks: (1) 10,000 steps = a 1965 pedometer name. (2) 3 days of 4,300 steps → post-meal sugar spikes nearly doubled. (3) 40 min 3x/week → hippocampus +2% in a year.
