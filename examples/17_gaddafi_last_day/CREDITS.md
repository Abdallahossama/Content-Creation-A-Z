# File 17: Gaddafi's last day (Sirte, Thursday 20 October 2011): credits

Real images only. Never shown: the capture, the ambulance, the meat locker, any body, wound or blood (the capture videos were never opened).
No women on screen: Gaddafi's female bodyguards stand behind him in most wide shots of 2000-2010, so every clip is cut to men-only seconds
checked frame by frame (research/assets.md section V; contact sheets build/contact/vclips_*.jpg), and Secretary Clinton is narrated, never shown.
