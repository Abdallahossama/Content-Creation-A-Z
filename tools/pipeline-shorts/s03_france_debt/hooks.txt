France now pays more to borrow money than Greece. Yes, that Greece.
You'd think Greece pays the most to borrow in Europe. This month, France does.
France owes 3.6 trillion euros, and this month it started paying more than Greece to borrow.
Your coffee in Paris costs more than in Athens. So does France's debt, for the first time in years.
Is France going bankrupt? No. But it now pays more to borrow than Greece.
