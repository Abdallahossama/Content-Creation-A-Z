You've probably never heard of this vote, but today, Alberta, a province bigger than France, votes on whether to start leaving Canada.
Alberta sits on the fourth-largest oil reserves on Earth.
And about ninety percent of its oil exports go to the United States.
There are ten questions on the ballot.
The tenth asks one thing: stay in Canada, or start the legal process toward a future vote on leaving.
So even a yes doesn't mean Alberta leaves. It only opens the door to another referendum.
And most Albertans want to stay.
The latest polling average: sixty-eight percent remain, twenty-six percent start the process.
But people are showing up. On the first day of early voting alone, over a hundred and seventy-four thousand people voted.
And Alberta isn't alone. On October fifth, Quebec voted its separatist party back into power.
But its leader says no independence vote while Trump is president.
So, is Canada breaking up? Not today.
But tonight, Canada finds out how many Albertans want out.
