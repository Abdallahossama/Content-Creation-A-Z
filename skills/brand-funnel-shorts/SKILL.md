# Brand Funnel Shorts (codebyabdullah)

The personal brand is **عبدالله أسامة / @codebyabdullah / codebyabdullah.com**: an Egyptian
software engineer who builds automation, AI and custom systems for Gulf SMEs, Arab founders and
agencies. Videos are filmed by him on camera, in light "white" Egyptian Arabic. This brand is
**completely separate from The Last Hours** (never cross-link, never mention one in the other).

Read `docs/10-PERSONAL-BRAND-PLAYBOOK.md` first: it holds the brand facts, services, the three PRODUCTS (Razqora, survey platform (name TBD), Jawwab · جوّاب) with their never-claim list, the edit pipeline and
every rule the owner approved or rejected.

## The weekly trio (content funnel)

Every week = 3 short videos, one per funnel stage. Post Sun / Tue / Thu, 21:00 KSA (20:00 Cairo),
Gulf work week. Same vertical file to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels.

| | 1. REACH «انتشار» (TOFU) | 2. TRUST «قيمة» (MOFU) | 3. CONVERT «تحويل» (BOFU) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job | Strangers stop scrolling and follow | Followers learn something they can use; "this guy knows" | Warm viewers comment / DM / book a call |
| Viewer | Business owner who never heard of him | Follows, hasn't talked to him | Has watched 3+ videos, has a real problem |
| Topics | Surprising money/time numbers, myths, hot takes on AI, "you're losing X" | How-tos, mini tutorials, frameworks, checklists, tool comparisons | Case studies (before/after), behind the scenes of a client build, offer, FAQ / objections, results |
| Length | 15–30 s | 30–60 s | 30–60 s |
| Pace | Fastest: cut every 1.5–2.5 s, B-roll every line | Calm teacher: 1 idea per beat, screen recordings | Story pace: problem → build → result, proof on screen |
| Tone | Bold, curious, a bit provocative, never insulting | Generous, clear, "خد دي مني" | Confident, specific, numbers and real screens |
| CTA | «تابعني» / save / share with a business owner | «احفظ الفيديو» + «اكتب شغّال وهبعتلك …» (lead magnet) | «اكتب شغّال» / «كلمني واتساب» / «احجز 20 دقيقة مجانًا» (link in bio) |
| KPI | Views, shares, follows, 3-s hold | Saves, follows, comments «شغّال» | DMs, link clicks, booked calls, WhatsApp chats |

Rule of thumb: while the account is under ~5K followers keep the trio 1:1:1 but pick the
REACH topic with the biggest audience; once leads come in steadily, swap one REACH for a second
TRUST or CONVERT. Never post two CONVERT videos in a row.

## Hook formulas per type (write 3, score, pick 1)

Use `yt-script`'s scoring (clarity, curiosity gap, specificity, speed to payoff) on 3 options.
First 2 seconds = the hook spoken AND shown as text. No intro, no logo, no «السلام عليكم يا جماعة».

**REACH**
- Money/time shock: «شركتك بتخسر ٩٠ ساعة في الشهر… وانت مش واخد بالك.»
- Myth bust: «الذكاء الاصطناعي مش هياخد شغل موظفينك. ده هياخد شغل اللي مش بيستخدموه.»
- Contrast: «نفس الموقع: واحد بـ ٥٠٠٠ ريال وواحد بـ ٥٠٠. الفرق ده.»
- Curiosity: «أغرب طلب جالي من عميل سعودي…»
- POV / list: «٣ حاجات في شركتك ممكن تتعمل أوتوماتيك النهارده.»

**TRUST**
- How-to promise: «إزاي تخلي واتساب شركتك يرد لوحده في ١٠ دقايق.»
- Mistake: «أكبر غلطة بتعملها لما تكلّم مبرمج عن فكرة تطبيق.»
- Framework: «قبل ما تبني أي سيستم: اعمل الـ ٣ خطوات دول.»
- Tool vs tool: «Excel ولا سيستم؟ إمتى تنقل؟»

**CONVERT**
- Before/after: «عميل في الرياض كان بيعمل ده بالإيد… شوف بقى.»
- Behind the build: «إزاي حوّلنا فكرة عميل لنظام كامل من A to Z.» (the first real reel)
- Objection: «"البرمجة غالية"؟ تعالى نحسبها.»
- Offer: «فاتح ٥ مواعيد تشخيص مجاني الشهر ده.»
- Result: «السيستم ده وفّر لعميل ٤ ساعات في اليوم.» (real numbers only)

## Script output (always deliver all of it)

1. **3 hooks** with scores; mark the pick.
2. **Spoken script** in white Egyptian, short lines (2–6 words), one line per beat, with timing:
   `0–2s HOOK / 2–8s PROBLEM / … / last 3s CTA`. Under 130 words for 60 s.
3. **Shot list** per line: `A` = face to camera, `S` = screen recording (what exactly to record),
   `C` = brand card (title + 3–4 bullets, made from `tools/personal-brand-reel/card.html`),
   `W` = his website/project screenshot, `B` = desk B-roll (typing, monitor, phone).
4. **On-screen caption words** + the ONE orange word per line (`#FF4F2B`).
5. **Post caption** (2–3 lines + question) and 3–5 hashtags (#برمجة #اتمتة #ذكاء_اصطناعي #السعودية …).
6. **Pinned comment** with the CTA keyword.
7. **LinkedIn version** (see below).
8. Filming note: the line he must say first (the hook) so the edit doesn't start mid-story.

## Visual rules per type (edit pipeline in the playbook)

- All: brighter neutral grade (never yellow), orange background lamp glow, denoise, clean voice
  -14 LUFS, Alexandria ExtraBold captions with one orange word, two audio versions
  (chill music + **halal** vocal nasheed beat).
- DEFAULT look = UI depth (v3): real-content panels BEHIND the head (hair overlaps a corner), small tags IN FRONT on the chest, one at a time, synced to the word (`tools/personal-brand-reel/depth/`). Never cover the face; no glass/gradient/glow/fake buttons.
- REACH: a card or screen on almost every line, punch-in zoom on every cut, whoosh on each card.
- TRUST: longer screen recordings with zoom on clicks, numbered step cards (01/02/03).
- CONVERT: real client/project screens, before → after split, the number on a card, end on his face
  saying the CTA (no B-roll in the last 2 s).

## LinkedIn (more professional, same videos re-cut)

- Post TRUST and CONVERT every week; REACH only if the topic is about business/money, not memes.
- Upload natively (never a YouTube link). Export **4:5 (1080×1350)** from the same edit: crop the
  9:16 around the face, captions and cards moved inside the 4:5 frame. 30–90 s.
- First 3 s = the business result or the number, not a joke. Keep the Arabic voice; add an English
  subtitle line under the Arabic one when the topic targets Gulf decision makers.
- Lighter edit: fewer whooshes, no meme sounds, music very low or none (use the halal version).
- Post text: hook line → 3–5 short lines of value → one question to the reader. Max 3 hashtags.
  Put links in the first comment, not in the post.
- Post Tue/Wed/Thu 08:00–10:00 KSA; reply to every comment in the first hour.
- Once a week turn the TRUST video into a text post or a PDF carousel (same cards) for reach.
- Profile: headline + About from the brand kit; featured section = booking link + 3 case studies.

## Honesty rules

Numbers on screen must be real or labelled «مثال». Client names, logos and screens only with
permission (his own projects on codebyabdullah.com are fine). Demo data in every screenshot.
Copy other creators' STYLE only, never their footage, words or music.
