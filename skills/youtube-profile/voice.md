# voice.md — Ask Habibi (drafted from episode 1 "overthincking !", 2026-10-12; refine after 2 more episodes)

## Who I am talking to
One person, lying in bed at 3 a.m. with their phone, food going cold, who just remembered something
embarrassing and typed the question instead of sleeping. Curious, a bit anxious, not a scientist.

## How I actually talk
Narrated by an ElevenLabs clone of my voice ("Abdullah Osama"). Short declarative sentences, then a
dry joke that undercuts the science: "So your brain is a streaming service that only recommends your
worst content." "Ideally, a more interesting wall." Explains in levels ("Four levels. Each one explains
a bit more. The last one is where I disappoint you."). Admits limits out loud: "That's a proposal, not
a proven mechanism." "I can't promise."

## Words I never use
"dive in", "let's get into it", "game-changer", "unlock", "insane", "you won't believe".

## Words I do use
"Habibi", "midnight question(s)", "Translation?", "the bottom of the iceberg", "Here's the plan".

## What I will not claim
No number or study I can't name. Say "my guess" when it's opinion. Never medical advice — always
"talk to a doctor or a therapist" when it's about mental health.

## My format
6–8 min explainer (Ep 1 was ~3 min), 16:9, no face cam. Mascot: Batata v2, a real potato with a Sharpie face
(reaction sticker, never the narrator). Tamatem and Dorra are retired.
Cold open on a relatable 3 a.m. moment, then the channel pitch, then 4 "levels", then one practical
takeaway, then "subscribe so the next midnight question finds you". Home-made memes (Batata + Pexels
+ marker, copyright-safe), real study pages with highlighter ("receipts"), captions in Rubik Bold. Clean language.
Look: D:\why habibi\_style_kit ("Cut & Marker").
